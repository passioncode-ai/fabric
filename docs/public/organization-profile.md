# PassionCode.ai

> **PassionCode.ai — A toolkit for AI-native teams.**
>
> From vibe coding to passion coding.

Tools for working with AI agents, with a shared design system and explicit product status.

## Switchboard

**Fabric Switchboard** is our first publicly downloadable product: a desktop beta for
managing AI-provider accounts. Open source under the GNU AGPL-3.0, with a commercial license
available; releases up to 0.4.0-beta.1 were released under PolyForm Noncommercial or Internal
Use, and earlier ones under MIT, and keep those terms. Choose macOS or Windows on the
[Switchboard page](https://passioncode.ai/switchboard/), where the release states its
installation requirements and verification limits.

[Explore Switchboard](https://passioncode.ai/switchboard/) ·
[Source and releases](https://github.com/passioncode-ai/fabric-switchboard)

## Inbox · development preview

**Fabric Inbox** is our desktop email product. Its Cloudflare and Gmail implementation
has local/synthetic acceptance; real-account acceptance and production deployment
remain unverified. Outlook and general IMAP are planned.

[Explore Inbox](https://passioncode.ai/inbox/)

## Fabric · in development

Fabric is the CEO AI agent we are building to coordinate agents and Projects.

**Stop managing agents one by one. Start operating projects.** A Project keeps its
purpose, team, authority, work, Evidence and history together while providers can change.
People decide what may act and what needs their judgment. Fabric’s private repository
also holds the technical kernel and compatibility contracts. Its signed macOS build is an early
preview, [downloadable from passioncode.ai/fabric](https://passioncode.ai/fabric/#download).
