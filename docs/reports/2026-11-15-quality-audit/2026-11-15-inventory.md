- Age: days from the earliest date written in the row (the register's "Raised at" is a stage name, not a date); rows from stages 0 to 2 without a date show no age (they date from 2026-08-16 to 2026-08-29).
- "Blocks": the row's own "latest it can be decided" when it names a release or gate; for board items, what the reporter said it blocks.
