## What the new Агенты must also handle (CO-419)

CO-419 (rail left, list by state on the right, the chosen agent's «Работа» and «Консоль» tabs in the centre) is the operator's decision and is not reopened here. Note: CO-419 is not in the `v0.3.12` carry-over (its «Next free ID» is CO-419), so this list is checked against the decision as stated in the brief. What the screen must carry so it does not repeat SX-3, SX-20 and SX-26:

1. **One list, every agent**, grouped by state: working now; quiet past its window («молчит»); idle with a console Fabric holds; ended but still held; joined from a terminal; disconnected («Отключён» with «Пустить снова»); coding agents installed but not running, not installed (with the install line) and needing sign-in, from Switchboard's catalogue that Настройки reads today; agents built in Fabric. Each state in words, never `running` / `idle` / `ended`.
2. **Reading, empty, failed, capped are four things**: «Ищу агентов…»; a group that is empty says so in its place; a failed read keeps the last list marked «как было в {время}» with «Повторить»; a capped `work.home` read keeps its «Прочитано не всё» note.
3. **Selection is stable** by session or agent id across reorders and refreshes (as `EstateAgents` held it), focus moves only on the person's pick (the 0.3.12 rule), and an agent that leaves the list leaves no page under its name.
4. **«Работа»** carries the task it holds (with «Открыть его задачу»), what it is doing, results, what it did by day, its project, and «Отключить» for a joined agent (SX-26), the proof line «Fabric не может доказать, что это Codex».
5. **«Консоль»** for an agent with no console Fabric holds (joined from a terminal, not running) says why there is none and what to do («Агент работает в терминале: его консоль там»; «Открыть консоль» to start one), never a blank pane.
6. **One home per terminal**: decide how the centre «Консоль» tab relates to SCN-172's tabs along the top (does opening it add a top tab, and does × on a top tab change the centre?), so one session never shows in two places with two states.
7. **The rail's dot and Главная's counts** read the same list (SX-7), so «агентов работает» on Главная equals the «working» group here.
8. **«Создать агента»** at the top opens «Создать» on its agent path (SCN-166 step 4).
9. **Narrow window**: the list over the centre under 850 px, with the chosen agent brought into view (the 0.3.12 rule).

- **Unknown is not zero, almost everywhere.** `EmptyState` requires `read` (`components/EmptyState.tsx:26`); counts show a placeholder, never 0, after a failed read (`HomeWork.tsx:70-76`); a kept reading says its time (`HomeWork.tsx:20-24`, `HistoryScreen.tsx:106`); «без активности» shows only when every reading it depends on answered (`EstateHome.tsx:297`).
- **One reading per number where it was applied.** The rail, the greeting, the «ждут вас» cell and each project's «Ждут вас» share one `board.query personOnly` (`App.tsx:774-777`); the strip and the lanes share `work.home` (`HomeWork.tsx:63-90`).
- **Failures in the person's words with the machine's beneath** (`FailureNote` on Главная, Агенты, the graph, the project page; `HistoryScreen.tsx:101-107`; Settings' pinned refusal, `SettingsScreen.tsx:398-403`).
- **Capped reads say so** (`HomeWork.tsx:27-32`, `BoardScreen.tsx` «Прочитаны только первые 100», `CommsAnnouncements.tsx:110`).
- **The way back** restores the place, the Board's view and tab, and the scroll (`LaunchShell.tsx:153-185`; `App.tsx:799-865`), with ⌘[ / ⌘] and the trackpad swipe, and focus moves to the new page's heading after a rail press (`LaunchShell.tsx:179-184`).
- **Console tabs**: a second open switches to the existing tab, × ends nothing, a gone session's tab leaves by itself, the neighbour comes forward (`App.tsx:871-904`).
- **The kanban explains its own limit** (the agent filter applies to «В работе» only, said on screen: `KanbanBoard.tsx:99`), and a column that could not be read says «?» rather than 0 (`KanbanBoard.tsx:108-109`).
- **The task page reads as a template** (what, why, what will be true), with an empty brief said in one sentence that tells the person what happens next (`TaskPage.tsx:280`; `i18n/ru.ts:1817-1818`), and «Как двигалась» naming who moved it.
- **Announcements never say «nobody owes an answer» on a failure** (`CommsAnnouncements.tsx:70-75`).
- **The graph lists every relation as text** under the drawing (`GraphScreen.tsx:104-109`), so nothing is only on the picture.
- **Plural forms** in the greeting and counts (`i18n/translate.ts#pluralForm`).
