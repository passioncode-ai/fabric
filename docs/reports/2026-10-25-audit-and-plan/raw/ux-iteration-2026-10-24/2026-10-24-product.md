## Summary

Fabric's parts are each better than they were a week ago, but the product as a whole asks the operator to hunt. One concept lives in several places under several names, the places the operator uses most (the task board, the conversation between projects) are not on the rail while rarely used ones are, and the project page has grown into two pages stacked on each other with most things said twice. The operator's walk asked for the same thing again and again in different words: say what is needed from me, in plain words, in one place, and show work as plan-level progress (OF-14, OF-18, OF-19, OF-21, OF-23, OF-24, D-4). The strongest improvements are therefore subtractive: one name and one home per concept, the board and Comms on the rail, a project page that says each thing once, one history, and the task as the object that carries its spec, agent, console and thread. CO-419 is the right anchor for 0.3.13: it makes the agent's task («Работа») the default view and the console secondary, which is D-4 applied to Агенты. It needs one guard: the console must have one home, or CO-419 recreates the multi-chat cockpit the anti-vision refuses.

## Where the shape fights the goal

### 1. «What is needed from me» has four names and three homes

The walk's first item is that the home must say what is needed from the person (OF-14), and «Нужны вы» first (OF-15). 0.3.12 unified the count (CO-391) but not the place or the name:

- Главная: «Нужны вы» (`i18n/ru.ts:1642`, `launch/HomeParts.tsx#HomeBoard`) and the stat cell «ждут вас» (`ru.ts:1676`).
- The project page: «Вопросы ко мне» (`ru.ts:1670`, `launch/ProjectLaunch.tsx:135`) and, lower on the same page, a second «Нужны вы» panel (`ProjectHome.tsx:430`, `BoardPanel` with `personOnly`).
- The Board: a mode «Нужны вы» (`ru.ts:1632`) with five tabs of its own: «Актуальное», «На проверке», «На следующий раз», «Разобрано», «Все темы» (`ru.ts:1323`, `:1371` to `:1374`; `shared/appRoute.ts:62` `BoardTab`). Its «На проверке» tab shares its name with the kanban's «На проверке» column (CO-418, iteration 3 UX-4).

The operator answers a question in place on Главная (CO-391), but reviews and proposals go to the Board's second mode, and on a project page the same items appear twice.

### 2. «Доска» means three things, and the one the operator looked for is not on the rail

The walk: the operator expected a kanban and «could not find or scroll it» (OF-19). The rail holds Главная, Агенты, История, Настройки and «Создать» (`launch/LaunchShell.tsx` `PLACES`), not the Board. The Board is reached from a stat cell, a lane's «Открыть доску» or a project's «Доска проекта» (`App.tsx:1286`, `:1342`). Meanwhile the word «доска» names:

- the task board by stage (`ru.ts:1790` «Доска», `:1643` «Открыть доску»);
- the questions board («Нужны вы», above);
- the board between projects, which the brand's own terminology forbids calling «доска» (`docs/brand/terminology.md:105`: «Never "the board" / «доска» in its text: the rail's Board / «Доска» is the task board»). The interface does it anyway: «Не удалось прочитать, что ждёт на доске проектов» (`ru.ts:1640`), «ветки доски между проектами» on the graph (`ru.ts:1293`, `:1303` to `:1305`), «записывает это на доске» in Settings' machine trust (`ru.ts:303` to `:305`). The terminology also says «the rail's Board», which the rail does not have.

### 3. The conversation between projects lives under Settings

Comms (SCN-143), one of the four things the brief says Fabric shows, opens from Настройки → «Ещё» → «Переписка между проектами» (`launch/SettingsScreen.tsx:377` to `:391`) or from a button inside the home's agents widget (OF-16). Its unread count is drawn on the rail's «Настройки» row (`LaunchShell.tsx`, `commsUnread` on `place === 'settings'`). A count of unread messages on Settings tells the operator nothing about where to look. The walk found Comms «unreadable» (OF-29); 0.3.11 fixed the reading (Лента) but not the finding.

### 5. History is in six places under six names

История («Что делали агенты» and the acts, `HistoryScreen.tsx`); Пульс («Видеть ритм и понимать состояние», `PulseScreen.tsx`, `ru.ts:1069`) with Releases behind it; «Журнал проекта» on each project (`ProjectHome.tsx:386`); the folded journal in Главная's «Ещё на этой странице» (`EstateHome.tsx:598`); «Прямо сейчас» on Главная's side (`HomeParts.tsx#HomeLive`; the same title as the stat strip's label, `ru.ts:1673`, `:1726`); and «Личная история и восстановление» in Settings (`ru.ts:839`), which is the private conversation history, not acts. История's filters (CO-414) are owed in 0.3.13; without them the operator cannot narrow the one place that should hold all of it.

### 6. The home still carries what the walk did not ask for

Главная has eleven blocks (`EstateHome.tsx:512` to `:636`): greeting, «Мой облик», «Ритм работы», the four stat cells, «Нужны вы», «Где остановились», «Ждут между проектами», «Кто что делает», «Мои проекты», «Ещё на этой странице» (profile and journal), and on the side «Агенты в работе» and «Прямо сейчас». The code says why some are there: «Not on the launch home of the design … Until those screens exist they stay here, folded, rather than disappear» (`EstateHome.tsx:595`). Those screens now exist (Pulse, История, Persona).

### 7. Twenty-one routes behind four rail places, one of them unreachable

`AppRoute` has 21 kinds (`shared/appRoute.ts:30` to `:103`). The rail reaches four plus «Создать»; SCN-162 is titled «the five places» and lists four (`docs/ux/scenarios.md`, SCN-162 step 1), and promises «every place … one press away from anywhere», which is false for the Board, Comms, the graph, Plan, Pulse and Releases. `where` (WhereWorkScreen) is rendered (`App.tsx:1222`) but no control sets it: its scenario SCN-141 is retired. Help is reached from Settings → Ещё and offers its own jumps to five screens (`HelpScreens.tsx`, `launch.help.go.*`). The graph alone has five names (0.3.12 review, iteration 2, UX-8).

### 8. Starting and watching an agent are split across places

The brief's core verbs are «start and watch coding agents». Watching is on Агенты (CO-393's agent page) and in console tabs along the top (OF-03, SCN-172). Starting is in a project's «Задача» panel at the bottom of the second page (finding 4), in «Создать», or not at all: SCN-166's «Консоль» for a free coding agent and «Создать агента» are not built (SCN-166 status; CO-418). CO-419's «Консоль» tab inside Агенты would add a second home for a console beside the top-bar tabs, and an agents list by state with live consoles is one step from the «multi-chat cockpit» of vision §6, unless the task stays the frame.

### 9. Nothing comes to the operator outside Fabric

OF-40 asked for a question or review to reach the person outside Fabric, once, with a link back. It moved to 0.4.0 (CO-413). Until then every «needs you» depends on the operator opening the app; the walk's other items make Fabric clearer to read but not more present.

## What fits 0.3.13 beside CO-419

0.3.13 already carries the contract's repin, one schema step, the tracking of turns, Settings' section pass, CO-414 and CO-418 (triage, `2026-10-23-release-0312-triage.md:30` to `:37`). Beside CO-419 these changes are small in code and large in effect, and most touch the same files CO-419 and CO-418 already open:

1. **With CO-419 itself:** PX-7 (one console home, «Работа» the default), PX-5's interface half (the «Работа» tab is the task page), PX-6 for a free agent's «Работа».
2. **The shell pass:** PX-2 (rail), PX-3 (one word per thing), PX-10's `where`. These also make SCN-162 true.
3. **The inbox and the home:** PX-1 and PX-9; they resolve CO-418's UX-4 and the «Повторить» items on Главная on the way.
4. **History:** PX-8's first half, built as CO-414.
5. **Project page, first cut:** PX-4's de-duplication only (one goal source, one agents list with people, one «Нужны вы», «Написать задачу» in place).
6. **At the repin:** PX-5's data half (CO-415, CO-416).

## Not now

- **PX-4's full split** of the project page into a page and a resources page: a rewrite of a 2 297-line screen with its tests; do it after 0.3.13 shows which sections the operator opens.
- **PX-8's second half**, folding Пульс and Releases into История: they carry the routines' cadence and the release preview, which need their own home decided first.
- **PX-11**, notifications outside Fabric (CO-413, 0.4.0): it needs a credential for the main process, the same gate CO-411 records.
- **CO-417's shared memory across stores** and **CO-409's wake when nobody answers**: agents-first work of 0.4.0 (P-16).
- **Ruled out by the anti-vision:** a customizable pipeline with columns the ladder does not enforce (D-1, and §6's «human task tracker with agents attached as assignees»); a grid of live consoles or a chat-first Агенты (§6, «multi-chat cockpit»); Fabric's own quota panel back (D-3: Switchboard owns it, and §6's «generic integration dashboard»); assigning tasks to agents by drag on the kanban as the main way to give work (the task is given in its Project with its spec, PX-6, not dropped on an assignee).

## Open review question

Did we plan this correctly? The concrete choice open to review is PX-1: putting reviews and proposals into the one «Нужны вы» inbox, rather than keeping «На проверке» as a separate place. It removes a mode and five tabs, but it makes the inbox longer on a busy day; the alternative is two rail counts («Нужны вы» and «На проверке»), which keeps two places for «what waits for me».
