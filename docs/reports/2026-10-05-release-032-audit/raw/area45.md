## What was checked and found sound

**Area 4 (error handling and observability).**
- **External calls carry deadlines.** Every `fetch` in `src/main` has one: analytics 15 s (`analytics.ts:215`), quota 8 s (`quota.ts:238`) and the product forwarder (`productForwarder.ts:119`); the only exception is the SMOKE-only probe at `index.ts:4269`. Every child process has a bound and kills its process group: `gitRun.ts:128`, `:199` (caller budget), the keychain read (`quota.ts:206`, 5 s SIGKILL), `observatoryVault.ts:58-90` (SIGKILL timer; the secret stays on stdin and is redacted from stderr by `said()`), `executorDetect.ts:171-200` and `executorAuth.ts:50-80` (group kill on every path), `projectFolder.ts:28`, and the loopback WebSocket handshake (`loopbackWsClient.ts:69-78`).
- **The ops sink is sound.** It never throws, counts and publishes lost lines (`ops.ts:69-121`), and redacts both detail and error message (`shared/opsLog.ts:25-31`, `:139-151`).
- **IPC and the surface are covered.** All IPC goes through `handle()` with a correlation id and duration (`index.ts:171-186`). The agent surface's request handler is not fire-and-forget: it records the failure and answers 500 or cuts the response (`agentSurface.ts:357-366`). Board RPC throws and unreadable answers are recorded (`boardService.ts:116-125`).
- **Agent-visible errors are scrubbed.** Product forwarder errors are scrubbed (`productForwarder.ts:191`), and executor spawn errors are withheld on purpose because they can carry private paths (`executorAuth.ts:51`).
- **No console channel and no direct `ipcMain.handle`** remain in `src/main` (`check-ops.mjs` reports only the two acpShell catches).

**Area 5 (duplication and drift).**
- **Region markers resolve.** `check-regions.mjs` reports 175 markers, all closed and resolving. I sampled hub-discovery, hub-external-ingress, hub-consent, hub-call, project-board-tools, project-board-service, acp-shell, mcp-stdio-bridge, helper-process, usage-analytics, usage-analytics-wiring and quit-coordinator against their target sections. ADR-0115 §1 matches `hub.ts` (document shape, 0600, atomic write, withdraw-only-own-pid). `docs/ANALYTICS.md` matches `analytics.ts`: batch 25, queue 200, 23 h, retry 60 s / 10 min, 400/404 dropped, hourly `refresh()` (`index.ts:642`). ADR-0106 §1's 10 s deadline and exit code 3 match `quit.ts:52`, `:57`.
- **The AGENTS.md lifecycle constants match the code:** `TICK_MS`/`NOTIFY_MS` 60 s (`index.ts:1952`, `:2915`), the 8 s drain (`managedStop.ts:58`, `nativeStopRuntime.ts:80`), quota polling 60 s (`App.tsx:54`), credential hold 10 min (`quota.ts:101`), hub port 47070 (`hub.ts:36`), and one listening socket on 127.0.0.1 plus `[::1]` (`agentSurface.ts:380`, `:397`). The surface's call budget of 120 per minute matches `iteration-1-modules.md:386`.
- **The gates that exist hold.** `surfaceTools.ts` equals the registered tools (`check-surface-tools.mjs`, 27/27). The contract fixture pin `d4c8831` matches ADR-0117 §2 (`SOURCE.json`). Schema-by-count is consistent: 79 migration files, contract 79/79, and migration 81's header explains why its suffix is 81. `check-registers.mjs` passes, and so does `check-docs.sh` (0 errors).
- **The design map is current.** Its stamp equals the recomputed source fingerprint once the audit checkout's symlinked `node_modules` are excluded (R-09).

Out of scope, for the lifecycle area: `acpShellMain.ts:28` starts the agent `detached: true` (its own process group) and handles only SIGINT. When the PTY closes, a SIGHUP or SIGTERM to the shell ends Node without running `stop()`, so the agent's survival depends on it exiting at stdin EOF.
