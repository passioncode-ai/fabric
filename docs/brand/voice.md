# Voice

## Axes

| Axis | The product IS | The product IS NOT |
|---|---|---|
| Confidence | specific, falsifiable and direct | sweeping, absolute or self-congratulatory |
| Register | technical language grounded in real work | marketing prose about artificial intelligence |
| Distance | one capable builder speaking to another | a vendor lecturing an audience |
| Humor | dry, rare and never load-bearing | memes, hype or personality in place of an answer |
| Density | compact, with every unfamiliar term introduced | compressed into insider shorthand |

## Narrative

- **Hero:** the person accountable for the project, designing how people and agents work together
- **Enemy:** managing agents one by one while project purpose, authority, state and proof live in the operator's head
- **Category transition:** from vibe coding — directing chats, prompts and tasks — to passion coding — operating durable Projects and their loops
- **Product role:** PassionCode.ai is the organization, whose toolkit is for AI-native teams (ADR-0090); Fabric is its product, the CEO AI agent in development, and Fabric's tools carry its name; Switchboard is the first product selected for public download, and Fabric Inbox is a desktop email development preview
- **Promise:** a Project can carry purpose, team, Routines, authority, Evidence and learning from zero to one and beyond

## Invariant in every language

- Lead with what the system enables, then explain how.
- People remain accountable; agents remain bounded by explicit authority.
- State current capability, planned capability and open decision as different things.
- Every public claim resolves to [`facts.md`](facts.md).
- Use one name for every product concept across every surface.
- Public copy uses only public facts and published components.
- The primary positioning appears before the secondary slogan.
- “Vibe coding” names the useful starting point; never use it as an insult to builders.
- “Passion coding” always resolves to Project-level operation, never to enthusiasm or a fruit pun.

## Voice references

- **Admired:** GitHub Docs for directness and progressive disclosure; Linear for concise product language.
- **Refused:** generic AI automation pages that promise autonomy without naming authority, evidence or failure modes.

## Failure mode

**Insider shorthand.** Technical nouns accumulate until a newcomer cannot tell what
the product does; or “passion coding” becomes a clever label with no operational
meaning. Honesty becomes a performance of limitations instead of a clear account of
the useful system. The check: a new reader can explain the shift from managing agents
to operating Projects after the opening paragraph without already knowing the category.
