## Decisions

- 2026-11-11, by the operator's order to finish the board's reliability and roll the update out everywhere, the Mac Studio
  included: `v0.3.18` is not tagged on its own. Its release commit `bbc90b7a` stays in `main`, and 0.3.19 carries it with
  CO-559 and CO-556; the open checks on rc.2 (the window checks of items 4 and 6, the Docker-stopped start, (e)) are run
  on 0.3.19's candidate instead, recorded in its ledger. Reason: a separate 0.3.18 rollout would install a build whose
  board dispatcher must stay off (CO-556) on both machines, the opposite of the order.
- CO-554 joined 0.3.18 on the operator's word (2026-11-09, session `5d9bed98`, as its row cites), and iteration 3 alone
  was run again on it (twice): its delta from iteration 3's first candidate is the dispatcher's two files and their
  tests, nothing iterations 1 and 2 fixed (ledger, *Iteration 3* and item 8; PL-36).
- REQ-1's 10 s is amended for 0.3.18 to "within 70 s of an abnormal end": launchd spawns a `KeepAlive` job at most once
  every `ThrottleInterval` (60 s in the plist), written before the N6 (b) run (iteration 3, PL-28).
- A failure in items 2 to 6 stops the close; the fix takes a new three-iteration review under a new `verifiedCommit`,
  and its rc is `v0.3.18-rc.3` (PL-30).
