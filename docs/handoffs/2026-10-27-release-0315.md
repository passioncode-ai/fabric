## Done

- **Built** on `feat/mockup-parity` in three waves ([the plan and gap maps](../reports/2026-10-27-mockup-parity/README.md)):
  Главная, the left column, the agent page and the console brought to the approved

  against `dbb69aa4`, 18 findings, none blocking (V3-1 to V3-18). Every finding is fixed or ruled with its row. Reports
  and `fabric-release-reviews/1` receipts under `docs/evidence/reviews/0.3.15/iteration-{1,2,3}/`.
- **The final candidate** `112779af` was read by a fresh verifier
  ([report](../reports/2026-10-27-release-0315/2026-10-27-final-candidate-verification.md), verdict «ready to tag»):
  every iteration 3 disposition holds, eight test files passed one at a time, the five documents gates exited 0. Two
  low findings, FV-1 and FV-2 (V3-19, V3-20).
- **The release commit** on `feat/mockup-parity` adds only release metadata after `112779af`: the version 0.3.15, the
  CHANGELOG heading `## 0.3.15`, the release gate with its receipts, the ledger, this handoff, the verifier's report,
  the merge log, the design map's entry and `main`'s workspace pin (merged, pin only).

- Iteration 3's receipts name the reviewed candidate `dbb69aa4`; the final candidate `112779af` is covered by the final
  candidate verification report declared in `releaseMetadata`, as 0.3.14 did.

  candidate and a new verification.
