# Release verification: Fabric 0.3.14, agents and sync, an emergency release, three independent iterations

0.3.14 is the operator's emergency release of 2026-10-26 (P-28, [plan and waves](2026-10-26-release-0314-plan.md)),
promised by 2026-10-29: an agent's tasks newest first on its page, each openable (the flow), reminders on every turn and
abandoned work returned or reviewed by the sweep (sync), and the simple Fabric's first part (wave 4: the left column of
projects and agents, Главная's numbers and «В работе» by project, the agent screen with its lanes, «Открыть консоль в B»).
The simple Fabric's second part is 0.3.15 (P-29, 2026-11-05); the organization slice is named by its date, 2026-11-21
(P-27).

**No schema step.** The local database stays at schema 98, as in 0.3.13: upgrading is the new bundle alone, and going
back is the 0.3.13 bundle alone, with no backup to restore ([CHANGELOG](../../../CHANGELOG.md), «Upgrading»).

## Protocol

The protocol is 0.3.13's ([0.3.13 ledger, Protocol](2026-10-26-release-0313-verification.md#protocol)), unchanged:
fresh reviewers per iteration, one per level (UX-n, ER-n, DO-n, DA-n, PL-n), a finding `V<iteration>-<n>` with a
disposition (**fixed** with its watched test, **ruled** with a register id, or **not a defect** with the measurement),
and iteration 3 ends with zero open blocking findings. Reviewers work read-only in a detached checkout of the candidate;
their reports are committed under `docs/evidence/reviews/0.3.14/iteration-N/` as written, with one
`fabric-release-reviews/1` receipt per level. A reviewed candidate is never rebased: `release/0.3.14` takes `main` by a
merge and lands on `main` by a fast-forward of that merge. Each iteration's code fixes and their tests are in the plan's
[«Review, iteration 1»](2026-10-26-release-0314-plan.md#review-iteration-1),
[«Review, iteration 2»](2026-10-26-release-0314-plan.md#review-iteration-2) and
[«Review, iteration 3»](2026-10-26-release-0314-plan.md#review-iteration-3); the rows below carry their dispositions.

Finding ids: iteration 1 numbers every level from 1; iteration 2 continues UX (UX-11 to UX-21) and ER (ER-13 to ER-21)
and numbers DO, DA and PL from 1 again; iteration 3 continues every level (UX-22, ER-22, DO-9, DA-7, PL-9 on).
