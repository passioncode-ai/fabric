# Fabric in an Aperture-like interface style (sketch, 2026-10-16)

The operator, 2026-10-16: bring Fabric's product and its website to the interface style of
[Aperture by Tailscale](https://aperture.tailscale.com); the brand stays. A first render for review, not a decision.

- **Kept from the brand:** the PassionCode mark, gold as the one action colour, dark surfaces, the semantic colours.
- **Taken from Aperture's style** (read from its page with WebKit, 2026-10-16): a neutral graphite background
  (`#141313`, Aperture's `#181717`) instead of the plum tint; the system font with large, medium-weight, tightly
  tracked headlines; hairline borders (white at 9–16 %); generous space; small uppercase section labels; a light
  secondary button; a faint grid behind the website's hero.
- **The app screen** follows [the 0.3.7 canvas](../README.md): five places on the rail, the projects list under them,
  questions on the home in plain words with their options as buttons, «Сейчас» per agent, an act with **Отменить**.

Files: [`fabric-style.html`](fabric-style.html) (self-contained), [`fabric-app.png`](fabric-app.png),
[`fabric-site.png`](fabric-site.png). Next: the operator's verdict; then `sheleg-design` takes the accepted direction
into tokens (a neutral-graphite variant of the PassionCode palette) and wave 1's prototype.
