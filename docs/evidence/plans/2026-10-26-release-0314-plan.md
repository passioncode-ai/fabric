# Fabric 0.3.14: agents and sync, an emergency release

**Status:** in progress, 2026-10-26 · plan row P-28 · target 2026-10-29 · branch `release/0.3.14`.

## Why

The operator, 2026-10-26, in this repository's session, verbatim: «Смотри, доска, вот наша фабрика, он пока скорее в
бесполезном больше состоянии. Типа мне, наверное, достаточно, чтобы мы у тебя в фабрике видели, какие у меня есть
проекты, видели, какие сейчас он у меня конкретно в работе агенты, типа и какая у него конкретно задача. Можно будет
перейти на агента, посмотреть его флоу, как он типа по задачам шел, желательно, чтобы это был список сверху, чтобы
сверху была самая последняя, а вниз уже предыдущая. На каждую задачу можно было развернуть, перейти к ней. И так вот,
это главная фишка фабрика, плюс синхронизация все-таки. То есть главное, чтобы работала она для агентов, для
синхронизации, чтобы они четко были, чтобы были ремайндеры, задачи были не брошены, все обновлялось, грамотно
заполнялись, ну короче, проработать максимально с этой части и выпустить апдейт сейчас экстренный.»

Numbering, agreed with fabric-workspace the same evening (a cross-session message, not on the board): this release
is 0.3.14; the organization slice, self-update (P-12, CO-364, CO-429) and the employee's install path (CO-219) move to
0.3.15 (P-27) with its date of 2026-11-21 unchanged. Its conditions: no new protocol (reminders and abandoned work stay
on DEC-0126 and `work.swept@1`), one sessions screen with Fabric Dashboards (MD-12; request `55db8ac8` on thread
`d683f99d`), one heavy run at a time on this machine.

Vision: aligned. It serves the alignment test's evidence trail: every row is a project's task with its project named,
openable to its page; it is not a multi-chat cockpit.

## What the operator's database showed (2026-10-26, read only)

A read-only audit of the live database at 18:13 UTC (SELECT only), with 0.3.13 installed:

- Tasks: 149, of which 145 have no brief section at all and none has why or expected; 115 sit in review (71 older than
  3 days) and none was ever closed; 2 are running with no live claim.
- Sessions since 2026-10-13: 951 enrolled, 243 ended, only 82 ever claimed a task, 14 ever sent a heartbeat; in the last
  24 h 19 sessions changed files, 18 of them holding no claim.
- The sweep of abandoned work (four receipts since the install) found what it should (5 lapsed claims, 44 unread board
  answers, 16 to 18 unmoved requests) and queued 66 reminders, but a reminder is delivered only in a `fabric_work_list`
  or `fabric_heartbeat` answer, and heartbeats run at about one a day.
- A lapsed release leaves a running task in running when another claim moved it there (`f0ca028a`); a `done` release
  that failed to move its task to review is never repaired (`b2c42b88`).

The screens (read-only audit of the code): projects are on the home already; an agent's task is its oldest live claim
(it shows a finished task, misses a Fabric-launched task and drops an untitled one); the agent page lists event types
without naming the task; the board's «В работе» keeps dead work and names an agent's type, not its session.

## Composition

| Wave | What | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · The flow | A session's tasks newest first on the agent's page, each expandable and openable; the agent's current task is its newest running one (claim, Fabric-launched task or stage report), untitled kept; history rows name their task; the board names a card's session, puts dead work in «Брошено» and pages «Готово» | SCN-158, SCN-166, SCN-173 |
| 2 · Sync | Reminders ride on every turn (`fabric_session_observe`'s answer carries the session's nudges); a session that changes files without a claim is reminded; a running task left with no live claim goes back to backlog whoever moved it, and a `done` release that left its task running is moved to review; a claimed task with an empty brief is reminded with its missing sections; `fabric_whoami` names the project's waiting requests and unread answers; review older than 3 days reaches the operator's queue | SCN-140, CO-427, CO-439 |
