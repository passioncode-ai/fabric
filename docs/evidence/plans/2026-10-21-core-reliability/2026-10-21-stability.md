## What works

- **Startup never dies silently.** Every refused start wrote `startup-failure.log` and a classified cause (`index.ts:5149-5200`); the schema gate refused an incompatible database instead of corrupting it (`schema-not-ready`, 89 vs 94).
- **The 60 s cycle leaves a receipt even when idle**, which is what made this outage timeline possible at all (`index.ts:2600-2631`; 9 616 `skipped_no_delta` receipts, 4 `outcome_unknown` that say why: `name resolution failed`, `fetch failed`, `TimeoutError`).
- **Database calls are bounded.** 15 s per main-process request (`dbDeadline.ts:15`), 5 s for hub credential checks (`accessStore.ts:26`); the one deadline that fired produced a 503 with `retry-after: 5`, not a hang and not an "unknown credential" (`agentSurface.ts:1080-1085`).
- **The quit deadline works.** The one quit that stalled ended at 10 s with exit 3 (`quit.ts:54,59`), as documented.
- **No renderer crash or unresponsive window in the surviving 12 h** (`render-process-gone` handler `index.ts:5289`, reload limit `rendererRecovery.ts:11`; zero `window.unresponsive` records), and no Fabric `.ips` crash report in seven days.
- **The ops logger did not lose records** (no `ops.gap` line), and failures carry correlation ids.
- **Current state is healthy**: hub pid 68675 alive and named by `hub.json` (`origin http://127.0.0.1:47070`), stack containers up 13 h (`supabase_db_fabric` healthy), schema 94, main process 130 MB RSS at 0.1 % CPU.
