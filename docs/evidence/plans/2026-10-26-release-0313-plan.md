## The 0.3.12 rollout and the move to Colima (the operator, 2026-10-26)

The operator decided to move this Mac from OrbStack to Colima at once (relayed by fabric-workspace: «давай сделаем
миграцию сразу всё пока еще сотрудники не начали ставить на colima»), in one window with the 0.3.12 rollout. Only the
volume `supabase_db_fabric` moves: Fabric runs db, rest, auth and kong
(`apps/desktop/src/main/stackServices.ts#STACK_EXCLUDED_SERVICES`). 0.3.12 follows `DOCKER_HOST`, so the installed
build can run on Colima before 0.3.13. The order: final dump; `supabase stop` on OrbStack with its volumes kept as
the rollback; fabric-workspace copies the volume; a rehearsal on the new dump; `DOCKER_HOST` set; 0.3.12 installed,
started through the broker, stopped, `migration up`, schema 96 checked; OrbStack stopped only after that.

First attempt, 2026-10-26 03:57Z: the dump `pre-0.3.12-colima-2026-10-26.dump` (schema 95, journal 27 729) and the
stop were done; then an agent's `ensure_running` reopened Fabric 0.3.11 at 03:58:40Z, which started the stack on
OrbStack again, and OrbStack began updating itself. The volume copy was called off. `fabric.desktop` was held in the
broker (policy revision 13), then released (revision 14) when the move was put off until the disk has 15 GB free and
swap is under 16 GB (fabric-workspace, 05:08 CET: the disk fell to 147 MB, the partial copy in Colima was deleted). The
hold is set again at fabric-workspace's signal, right before the person's Cmd+Q.
