1. **"Add N as projects" is not sticky; a documented claim is false** — UI · blocking. With 42 repositories the footer sits at 4620 px in a 900 px viewport. The nearest scrolling ancestor is `MAIN.content` (overflow auto, height 4685, never scrolls); the real scroller is `DIV.app-main`, so `start.css` `position: sticky; bottom: 0` never engages. SCN-128 step 3 says sticky. Second symptom: the window can scroll past the top bar; a scrollIntoView pushes it off screen and leaves a blank band.
