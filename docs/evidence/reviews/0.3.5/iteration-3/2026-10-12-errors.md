## Summary

11 findings, **0 blocking** and 11 non-blocking. The four iteration-2 blockers hold: `release.yml` parses, the schema check reads the real client's answer, Fabric's own 4xx refusals keep the session, and eviction picks the least recently active session. They leave residues in the session lifecycle, though:

- **ER-1.** The cap of 8 enrolled sessions per binding is machine-wide per runner. An evicted fabric-mcp re-enrolls silently on its next call, so a ninth working agent sets off a rotation: every round ends another working session and releases its claims.
- **ER-2, ER-3, ER-5.** A session is still not left when fabric-mcp dies by a signal, when two session-hook rejoins overlap, or when a transport failure makes the proxy drop the session.
- **ER-6, ER-7.** Two link paths still say a failed or ambiguous read as an absence.
- **ER-8.** The ER-8 receipts of both earlier iterations sit in a test that no gate runs.

None of these breaks a security boundary, loses data, or hangs the app. In each case where an agent loses its session, the agent is told so.

## Rulings checked

In each case the agent and the screens are told who holds a task (`life: lapsed`, the holder's session). The operator can release a claim from the project page. ## Boundaries checked and found sound

- DELETE needs both the bearer and the session's `mcp-session-id`. It comes after the Host and Origin checks and never reaches the external door (`!leaving` before `handleExternal`). A rejoin or launched bearer that leaves takes only itself.
- The door token still cannot get a session: `enrollSession(null, …)` answers `needs-grant` or `no-session`.
- fabric-mcp sends a credential only after `moved(hub)` has re-read hub.json and its pid, before every enroll, ask and poll. `locateHub` refuses a non-loopback origin, a token file outside the root, a FIFO or symlink, and a dead pid.
- Logs carry no secrets. `bearerDigest` is the only form of an ended bearer kept; `link.dropped` and `link.open` carry no URL; the eviction record has ids, the cap and the idle seconds; fabric-mcp's stderr carries the SDK and hub messages, which never include the Authorization header.
- Bounded waits:
  - session-end releases at quit wait at most 3 s;
  - the quit deadline is 10 s, and the outside reaper fires at 15 s and checks the pid's identity on POSIX;
  - the leave DELETE is bounded at 2 s;
  - the git worktree probe is bounded at 3 s and the remote read at 3 s;
  - the schema check has a 10 s deadline, and a late answer grants nothing;
  - hub calls use the SDK's 60 s request timeout.
- Startup failures are typed (`SchemaWhy`, `SchemaUnverified`), and the per-OS remedies use the right stack folder. The `behind` remedy links a public repository.
