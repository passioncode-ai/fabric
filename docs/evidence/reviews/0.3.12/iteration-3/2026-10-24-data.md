## Not findings

- **Rebuild equals live for everything 0.3.12 adds.** At 96 the copy's projection and a fresh chain's rebuild of the same journal agree on `projects` (all 72 rows, profile columns included) and `project_relations`, before and after the business probes. `apply_project_profile` depends only on the event and the row, `append_event` projects in `seq` order under the estate's advisory lock, and the business rule is decided before the append, so the journal holds only events that were allowed and replays to the same values.
- **The owner read cannot leak across estates or projects.** It filters `estate_id` and `project_id` (the session's own) explicitly on the service client; `readProjectFacts` filters `estate_id`; `check-scope.mjs` now counts 18 raw queries, each narrowed to an estate. A business comparison is case-insensitive in the tool and the wall, and the per-project lock lower-cases its key.
- **The owner read's cost.** It is a seq scan with `limit 1` (1.69 ms at 21 861 events), run only when an agent's profile call names a business, typically once per onboarding. The analytics keyset pages are seq scans of 6 to 8 ms; the journal index for them is already ruled CO-418 (V1-31).
- **The wall's reads are bounded.** Projects: 1 000 a page, at most 20 000, then a logged miss; threads: at most 50 pages of 100; both under one 5 s deadline per post, whose abort reaches the request (`fetchWithDeadline` combines the caller's signal with its own). A miss lets the post through and is logged, as W2-5 decides.
- **The lock does not leak.** A chain is removed when its last link settles and is still the current one; a rejected link does not hold the next (`then(work, work)`); a call that names no business takes no lock and cannot change the business column (`case when e.payload ? 'business'`).
- **Grants and RLS of `project_relations`** on a live-shaped copy: RLS on, the member read policy, `authenticated` SELECT only, `anon` none. `service_role` also holds REFERENCES and TRIGGER through the schema's default privileges besides the migration's explicit grant; its write grants are iteration 1's DA-7, ruled CO-418.
- **The 0.3.12 Comms marks bound** (5 000) equals the heads read's bound and sits under the RPC's clamp (10 000).
