## Not findings

- **ER-1 of iteration 1 (business hides the offer).** Holds: setting only the business no longer hides «Познакомиться с проектом» (`ProjectProfile.tsx:64`; `ProjectProfile.test.tsx` passes). ER-9 above is the remaining edge.
- **ER-2 of iteration 1 (agent controls the wall's input).** Holds in the hub: an empty business is refused, a different one is refused with the person's route, the same one is dropped, an unreadable current business refuses a business but lets the other fields through and is logged (`agentSurface.ts:2614-2618`, `projectOnboarding.ts:140-157`; `project-onboarding.test.mjs` 8 passed). ER-2 above is the race left.
- **ER-3 of iteration 1 (people from Git).** Holds as stated: a failed reading is not cached, concurrent reads share one promise, and an unread history is said with its folders instead of «no commits» (`gitPeople.ts:120-133`, `ProjectProfile.tsx:125-149`). ER-6 above is the cost.
- **ER-4 of iteration 1 (redaction).** Holds (probe 5). `secret = correcthorsebatterystaple` (a bare identifier with spaces around `=`) and `password: hunter2` (lower case with `:`) are kept: neither was redacted by v0.3.11 either (the lower-case rule and the bare keyword are new in 0.3.12, `redact.ts` comments), so nothing regresses.
- **ER-5 of iteration 1 (stack start).** Holds on the ordinary path: no try starts with under `waitMs + minTryMs` left (`stackStartRetry.ts:178`), a killed try names its limit and keeps `killed`/`signal` for `classifyCause` (`nameTheLimit`), a quit's SIGTERM is not renamed (`env.ts:201`), and the failure title no longer says four minutes. ER-7 above is the Docker path.
- **ER-6 of iteration 1 (onboarding filing).** Holds: the read is inside the `try` and logged through `failed` before the throw, a missing brief part is written on the next press, presses for one project are chained per project (a rejected one does not block the next: `.then(file, file)`), and the chain entry is deleted once settled. Admission and the page use the same filer instance (`index.ts:968,981`).
- **ER-8 to ER-12 of iteration 1.** Hold: «Ждут ответа» names its window (`CommsFeed.tsx:126-127`); the agent filter applies to «В работе» only and says so (`KanbanBoard.tsx:62-65,99`); analytics read newest first with `capped` said (`ProjectAnalytics.tsx:52`) and questions oldest first with an exact count; a credential presented in the last 5 minutes, or any collected one in the first 5 minutes after a restart, is never swept (`accessService.ts:631-634`; `authenticate` is the only credential door, `agentSurface.ts:1266`); a sideways event something can scroll spends the gesture (`swipeTravel.ts:25`).
- **Business wall with upper-case ids.** The thread id is lower-cased before `participantsOf` (`boardService.ts:524-527`), and `board_get`'s `thread` is the database's lower-case uuid, so `t.id === threadId` cannot miss on case.
- **The reply's thread read.** `board_get` is `stable` and records nothing (`20261012000103_project_board_lifecycle.sql:568-577`), so reading a message's thread for the wall marks nothing read.
- **Comms marks bound (V1-38).** 5 000 marks validate in 0.3.12; a settings file with more than 4 096 marks read by 0.3.11 after a rollback would drop all marks (v0.3.11 `appSettings.ts:143`), which only an estate with more than 4 096 threads can reach; not a release risk here.
- **Onboarding queue memory.** The per-project chain is removed when it settles and is still the latest (`projectOnboarding.ts:103-105`); no growth.
- **The two loosened timing tests.** `hub-access-service.test.mjs` CO-206 still asserts the deadline's own message ("did not answer within 60 ms") and every signal aborted, so a store ignoring its deadline still fails; `hub-call.test.mjs` ER-13 still fails a forward that runs to its end (8 s against `< 6000`).
- **`useHome` retry.** The returned object is new each render, but no effect depends on it (`EstateHome.tsx:179`, `ProjectLaunch.tsx:79` read fields only); after a failed read the last view is kept and said with its time, and «Повторить» shows only when nothing was ever read (`HomeWork.tsx:307-316`).
- **Detached windows and the countdown.** The notice and the console share a column (`App.tsx:913-914`); the ticking line is `aria-hidden` and the announced line changes only with a new reading (`StackHealthNotice.tsx:64-73`).
- **Board view in the route.** `onMode` replaces the route's view and the screen syncs to a route-driven change once (`BoardScreen.tsx:63-68`, `App.tsx:1045-1056`); no update loop (the effect returns when `initialMode` equals the last one seen).
- **Plural forms.** `pluralForm` maps any non-`one`/`few` category (including fractions and 0) to `many`, so a missing key cannot be asked for in either locale (`translate.ts:27-30`).
