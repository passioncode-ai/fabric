## Not findings

- The 0.3.11 UX findings this release set out to answer are answered in the code: the side column heading no longer breaks (`rail.css:84` is `overflow-wrap:break-word`), compact agent rows are one line (`launch.css:616`), the kanban's header follows its mode (`BoardScreen.tsx:193-209`), «Открыть на доске» passes its row (`HomeParts.tsx:140`), «Нужны вы» on the home lists the person's part and names the asker, История's agents rows are by day with who did them, the lanes open the task, the quota cell and its 60 s read are gone (`App.tsx` diff), the empty brief has a sentence, the task's way back names its place.
- Strings: en and ru hold the same 2702 keys; `check-design.mjs` passes; no em dash in any added string, CHANGELOG line or added scenario, flow or ledger text.
- The splash speaks ru or en from Settings and fits its 380 x 120 window with the longest lines (`splash.mjs`); the stack's own lines stay in the stack's words, by design.
- The graph has no sideways scroll at 1280 or 900, its node buttons show a 2 px focus outline, the light theme reads at 17.45:1, and the relations are also listed as text under the drawing.
- The Board: a picked row's detail takes focus (`BoardScreen.tsx:176-183`); the mode group and the list have their own accessible names; the agent filter keeps a remembered agent visible while it holds no card; «Показывать отменённые» adds a fifth column and says when the cancelled tasks cannot be read.
- Settings: a refused write is said at the screen's foot in a sticky `role="alert"` with «Скрыть» (`SettingsScreen.tsx:377-382`) and no longer as the shell's banner; «Этот компьютер» has a hint per value tied by `aria-describedby`; the catalogue's refusal and 5-second timeout are said apart from a failure.
- Comms' «Объявления» has reading, unavailable with «Попробовать снова», empty, all-answered and capped states as SCN-178 lists; the feed's «Ждут ответа» filter and the short-feed note render as the CHANGELOG says.
- The swipe never fires over something that can still scroll sideways, nor in an editor or a console (`App.tsx` `canScroll`, `swipeTravel.ts`).
- Not verified here: the launcher doctor's own `detail` and `fix` texts (their language and wording) need the network and the real launcher; the strings `estate.quota*` (ru.ts:79-81, 182) are now reached by no component but pass the gate through the `estate.` prefix, which is housekeeping, not something a person sees.
