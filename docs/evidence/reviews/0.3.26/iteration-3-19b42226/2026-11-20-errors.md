## Checked and fine

- **The reducer never stays busy.**
  - Every path through `go` calls `onBusy(false)` before it reads `r`: the success branch at line 71 and the rejection branch at line 75. That includes a malformed answer (scratch test 4).
  - `'done'` always clears `busy`.
  - A `'busy'` dispatch for an ask that is no longer shown is ignored (`continueQueue.ts:31`, plus a test).
  - A `'busy'` dispatch with `null` cannot reach another ask, for two reasons. Cancel is disabled while a press is busy, and nothing but `'done'` or an idle `'link'` replaces the ask. So an instance never unmounts with its press still in flight.
- **Queue identity.**
  - A link for the busy session is not queued.
  - The newest waiting link wins.
  - `key={continueAsk.session}` gives B a fresh instance with no inherited `busy` or `refused` state.
  - A link for A's own session, arriving while A is idle after a refusal, drops a queued B. The run-three code behaved the same, and the `waiting` sentence disappears with it, so nothing false is said.
- **The `latest` ref** is assigned during render. Its callbacks close over the stable `dispatchContinue` and the keyed session, so a render React discards cannot dispatch the wrong session.
- **No act can signal the wrong process on the new UX-41 path.** A Switchboard-only id reaches `find`, which re-reads live Claude files. A Fabric-owned process yields `null`, and `runningClaudeIds` refuses `not-outside`. `unknown-owner` still refuses. `resumeEnded` signals nothing. A concurrent close and continue of the same session are serialised by `continuing`.
- **`rowsChanged`** wraps only close and continue, keeps `rows`, and runs on every outcome through `finally`.
- **i18n:** `links.continue.close`, `goneHome` and `whichEnded` are in en and ru, and `viaSwitchboard` carries `{folder}` in both.
- **Ledger rows** V3-ER-26 and V3-ER-27 match the code, and their "red before" claims are confirmed.
- **CHANGELOG** «a continue link that arrives while another is being answered waits and is shown after it»: true at the candidate (scratch test 1).
- **Tests (from `apps/desktop`):**
  - vitest on `continueQueue`, `ContinueSessionAsk` and `MachineSessions`: 3 files, 34 of 34 pass.
  - `test/session-resume.test.mjs`: 15 of 15 pass.
  - `tsc --noEmit` exits 0 for `tsconfig.node.json` and for `tsconfig.web.json`.
