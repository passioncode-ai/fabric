# Fabric 0.3.26 release review, iteration 1: errors

Level errors, candidate 314c956660b35a25b4b71e3ba97cbd8b548914a4, baseline 53e8c3e2, reviewer Claude Opus 5.5 (claude-opus-5-5), read-only.

## Verdict

**Not releasable as is.** Two findings block the release.

1. The release's own test gates are red on the candidate.
   - `test/ceo-agent.test.mjs` does not parse, so none of the CEO stop-hold regression tests run.
   - vitest, which `ci.sh fast` runs, has 9 failures in 3 files, all caused by this release's changes.
2. «Сессии вне Fabric» and the rail's «На этом Mac вне Fabric» can list Fabric's own consoles as if they ran outside Fabric. «Закрыть» or «Продолжить в Fabric» on such a row then sends SIGTERM straight to a process Fabric holds (the CEO's console included). This skips the stop runtime, the person-stop hold and the launch journal.

The other findings are about load, bounds and claims that are not quite true. None loses data on its own.

## Checked and fine

- **`terminal end` conflict resolution** (`main/index.ts` ~5226). `personStopping` and `stopAsked` are added synchronously before the stop. The `try/finally` always calls `personStopEnded`, a thrown stop still settles the CEO's hold, and `stood` is read before the stop. History's stop uses the same `try/finally`.
- **The CEO's stop that holds** (`main/ceoAgent.ts`). The door counter, `stopDoorId`, `personStop` and `waitPersonStop` stay consistent across the start-in-flight, opening-console, two-door and message-during-stop paths. `fallBack`, `startFallback` and `onOutput` are gated on `held`. The only leak of `doors` needs the session id to change between a door's start and end while the hold is already set. I traced it and found no reachable ordering, because the native stop dedupes `pending` attempts. (Its tests do not run until ER-1 is fixed.)
- **Process signalling through the stop runtime.** `signalProcess` targets the captured process identity under `local(a)`. `dismiss` still needs `!running`, `closedRecorded` and `exitFinalized`. `processGone` widens only the termination states that `nativeStopRuntime` emits after the whole tree went quiet.
- **No shell injection.**
  - `claude --resume` takes its id from the `UUID_FILE` regex and passes it as an argv element; `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` comes from `claudeConfigDirs`.
  - `git` runs through `gitRun` with deadlines and SIGKILL.
  - `switchboard` runs through `execFile` with no shell.
  - Voice sample file names come from a fixed voice list, and the files are written atomically with tmp and rename at 0600/0700.
- **Retries.** `withOneRetry` retries only read-only switchboard calls (`readOnlySwitchboardCall`); a `sessions end` or `continue` is never retried.
- **IPC exposure.** Every new channel has a window rule: the machine acts and `resumeLast` are main-window only, and `ceoAgentStop` is main and CEO only. Session-id arguments are validated.
- **Launch journal (pure half).**
  - Parsing tolerates a torn line, and an end can fold in before its opening.
  - `chainOf` is bounded and safe against cycles.
  - Files are pruned after 13 months.
  - The runner-session watch stops at end and at quit.
  - The quit flushes the journal before the surface stops, inside the existing 10 s deadline.
- **Voice call** (`renderer/src/ceo/voiceCall.ts`). The level buffer is capped at 64, `stop()` releases the microphone tracks and the AudioContext, a failed `receive` is caught, and the sample fetch has a 20 s abort that also covers the body.
- **Tests run on the candidate.** `session-launches.test.mjs` 12/12, `session-resume.test.mjs` 7/7, `native-stop-runtime.test.mjs` PASS, `pty-launch-sink.test.mjs` 2/2.
