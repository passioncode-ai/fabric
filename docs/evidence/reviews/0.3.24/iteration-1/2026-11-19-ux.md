- **Level:** ux (what the person sees and does)
- **Candidate:** `b2e350d3500ca599343bdd79537e87eb5a286463` (detached), checkout `<worktrees>/fabric-0324-i1`

  - output `You've hit your limit · resets 5pm` ten minutes after the start: runner became `hermes`, `claude-code` set aside `why: limit`;

- Machine load during the run: 46 to 51.
