## Not findings

- **The heal on every watch try.** After a try that returned, the heal costs one `supabase status` (30 s limit) on a healthy stack. A status that fails for any reason other than the missing keys makes the try record `failed` though the start ran; the watch's next database read still brings the stack back, so only the record differs. The heal never runs when the try throws (Docker away past its 90 s wait, a refused start), and it runs at most once per try under the watch's `recovering` guard; a heal of up to about seven minutes (status 30 s, stop 120 s, start 240 s, status 30 s) only delays the next try.
- **CLI 2.114 and `created`.** The CLI's start treats a `created` Postgres container as not recoverable and reports status; Fabric reads `created` as `stopped`, starts, and the heal then stops and starts once. Rare (an interrupted first start), and the volume is kept by `supabase stop`.
- **The launch's «already running» swallow.** With CLI 2.114 the words are never thrown (it exits 0), so the catch is reached only under an older CLI, where the heal's own status read then decides.
- **The sweep's other paths.** One sweep at a time; every read in its own try; a blind read keeps its kinds' tellings and findings; SW-8 releases only on two readable sweeps, with the runs read and each release read back; nudges are taken only after the board read (`fabric_work_list`) or after the beat is journalled (`fabric_heartbeat`); a nudge carried by an answer the transport then loses is told again after 30 minutes. A failure of the whole sweep is logged and the cycle's receipt is still written (`main/index.ts:2829-2834`).
- **The hub's decision and mandate.** A malformed one is refused `decision-invalid` before anything is sent or remembered; the args hash covers both; an operator refusal passes only after `boundedProductOutput`, and its key is forgotten on the callee's word that it ran nothing, which is the contract's (C3.5). A decision is asserted by the calling agent and only checked for shape; whether it is the operator's is the callee's to judge (DEC-0121, DEC-0126). The span's `outcome: 'refused'` with the callee's code passes migration 80's key-only check.
- **Folder trust IPC.** `settings:folderTrustLocked` is `MAIN_ONLY` and read-only; the PTY and the screen read the same predicate.
- **The nightly gate.** As iteration 1 found; unchanged since.
