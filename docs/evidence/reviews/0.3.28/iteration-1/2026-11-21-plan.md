## Verdict
**Not ready. 1 blocking finding, 12 non-blocking.**

**What works:**
- **The merges hold together.** The four branches are fully merged (`git merge-base --is-ancestor origin/<branch> HEAD` is true for each).
- **Conflict choices hold.**
  - The 0.3.27 section of CHANGELOG and every older section are byte-identical to the tag's.
  - Each branch's lines sit under `## 0.3.28 (unreleased)`. The dock branch's "update in place" line was dropped, and that is correct because 0.3.27 already ships it.
  - The installer files are identical to 0.3.27's (`git diff v0.3.27 HEAD -- scripts/install-mac-update.mjs scripts/lib/install-mac-update.mjs scripts/test/install-mac-update.test.mjs` is empty). That is the right side to keep: the branch's copy lacks 0.3.27's `fabricRunning` (DO-75).
- **No schema step.** `supabase/migrations` ends at 130 (`board_operator_message_via`), the same as 0.3.27.
- **The operator door is safe to carry.** It is outside A-3's list, but it is dormant and tested.
- **Checkers and tests are green.**
  - `node scripts/check-registers.mjs` exits 0.
  - `node scripts/check-design-map.mjs` exits 0.
  - `node --experimental-strip-types --test` over operator-door, people-ask-run, memory-read, check-build-private and release-gate: 60 tests, 60 pass, 0 fail.

**Blocking:**
- PL-1: the release notes' opening sentence names the wrong set of branches. Several branches that were finished and checked before the pause are left out, and the train carries commits made after the pause.
