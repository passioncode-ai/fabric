## Verdict
**Not ready. 1 blocking finding, 9 non-blocking.**

**What works:**
- All six branches the train names are fully in the candidate: `git rev-list --count 618551f7..origin/<branch>` prints 0 for `fix/memory-source-kinds`, `audit/owner-hardcode`, `feat/dock-icon-face`, `refactor/board-operator-door`, `feat/approval-modes` and `feat/ceo-access`.
- The queue's order (`8cbf93ff`, schema 104) is in. The merge base with `origin/main` is `8cbf93ff`.
- `feat/ceo-session-windows` (14 non-merge commits) and `feat/ceo-triage` stay out, as the ledger says.
- These checks pass on the candidate: `check-registers.mjs` (608 carry-over rows, 374 open), `check-plan-ids.mjs`, `check-design-map.mjs` and `check-regions.mjs`.
- `pty-settle.test.mjs` passes 5 of 5, and `quit.test.mjs` fails nothing.
- Iteration 1's five receipts are closed, and their report hashes match.

**The blocking finding:**
- PL-14: CHANGELOG says "The owner chooses how much he is asked", but 0.3.28 has no control that sets the approval mode. Nothing records that the owner's home switch is missing.
