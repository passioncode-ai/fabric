## Verdict
**Not ready. 1 blocking finding, 8 non-blocking.**

What works:
- The single test files for the focus areas pass on the candidate:
  - `pty-settle` 5/5, `quit` 24/24, `operator-door` 5/5
  - `task-rank-write` 14/14, `task-rank` 23/23
  - `approval-mode` 3/3, `behaviour-journal` 3/3, `ceo-access` 8/8, `effect-tools` 7/7
  - `people-ask-run` 25/25, `board-cycle` 4/4
  - vitest `src/shared/peopleAsk.test.ts` 31/31
- The CO-625 settle runs in the right place in the ordinary quit: after the drain, before the last `app.quit()`. In that path it waits for exits to reach JavaScript, which is the moment node-pty's thread-safe exit call runs (`node-pty/src/unix/pty.cc#SetupExitCallback`, `BlockingCall`).
- `setPeopleChoice` refuses to write over a routes file that does not read.
- The operator door's bad name answers 400. Its stop closes the listener in a `finally`.
- A database at an unknown approval mode reads as `important`, and a settings patch with a bad mode is refused.
- A failed read of the queue's order is said on the board, in `fabric_work_list` and in the night brief.
- An account refusal for the CEO says why.

The blocking finding:
- ER-12: when the routes file exists but does not read, the people-ask bridge ignores the person's «Не отправлять в Telegram» and chosen targets. It then sends questions to the organization agent found by its live responder.
