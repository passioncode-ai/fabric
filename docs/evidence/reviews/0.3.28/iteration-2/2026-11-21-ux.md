# Fabric 0.3.28 release review, iteration 2: ux
Level ux, candidate 618551f73bd0ca272b3f2884ae33872607b1297d, baseline v0.3.27, reviewer claude-opus-5-5, read-only. HEAD before/after 618551f73bd0ca272b3f2884ae33872607b1297d/618551f73bd0ca272b3f2884ae33872607b1297d, dirty files before/after 0/0.

## Verdict
**Not ready. 2 blocking findings, 7 non-blocking.**

What works:
- **The queue's order on the Board.**
  - Each queued card says why it stands where it does.
  - «Выше» and «Ниже» pick the right neighbour (`KanbanBoard.tsx`, `move(x.taskId, cards[at - 2]?.taskId ?? null)`).
  - A drop takes the target's place from above or below.
  - Only one act runs at a time, with «Сохраняем порядок…» shown meanwhile.
  - With all projects shown, nothing can be dragged or moved.
  - The ru and en files each carry the same 23 `launch.kanban.order.*` and `launch.kanban.proposal.*` keys. The new strings have no em dash.
- **The routes table.** When `people-ask-routes.json` does not read, a line above the table says so and the selects are disabled (`SettingsScreen.tsx:258`, `:272`). Iteration 1's ER-1, UX-6 and DA-1 hold.
- **The Dock.** It follows the face only after the look was read with no problem and `chosen` is true (`dockIcon.ts#dockFollows`). A save after a failed read still turns following on (`persona.tsx` `read: s.read || r.saved`).
- **The CEO's account choice.** It refuses with the accounts that can be used, and tells a wrong provider, an account turned off, one used elsewhere and a wrong group apart (`ceoTools.ts#accountFor`). The voice knows `session_accounts` and `session_wait` by their voice names.
- **Tests run on the candidate, all green:**
  - `npx vitest run` on KanbanBoard, SettingsScreen and dockIcon: 3 files, 66 passed.
  - `node --experimental-strip-types --test`: task-rank 23/23, task-rank-write 14/14, approval-mode 3/3.

Blocking:
- **UX-12.** No person can choose an approval mode. No screen, CEO tool or voice tool sets it, yet CHANGELOG says «The owner chooses how much he is asked».
- **UX-13.** A queued task that waits for an open task never moves when dragged or moved with «Выше». The press still pins every card above it as «Место задали вы», and the column's note promises your placed tasks come first.
