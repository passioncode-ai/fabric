## Not findings

- **The newest-posts search can still come back one short on an extreme board.** With 99 posts spread over 4 000 000 journal numbers after a dense block of 1000, the 14 passes run out and the feed shows 99 of 100 (38 calls). Spans of 30 000 to 400 000 return 100 of 100, and the operator's whole journal is 19 122 numbers, so this needs a board two orders of magnitude older than today's. Recorded for CO-405's descending operator read, not this release.
- **A «Try again» pressed while a read is still running does nothing until the next 15 s tick.** The guard drops the tick rather than queueing it; the next poll reads, so the feed is at most 15 s late. Bounded and intentional (V2-3).
- **`apps/desktop/package.json` still says 0.3.10.** The release commit bumps it (Release close not started); not a data defect at this candidate.
- **The first full read per Comms opening.** Opening Comms on «Лента» costs `threadPage`, `responders`, `pending`, `heads` and, on the operator's board, 2 `list` pages: 6 reads, then 3 per 15 s. The rail's own `heads` read every 5 s is unchanged from 0.3.10.
- **`readSettingsState` on every `fabric_whoami`.** A synchronous read of a small JSON file per call, as before; an unparsable file is quarantined once by `localState.ts` and then answers `unreadable` (no language) until the person recovers it, which is what `operating-surfaces.md` says.
- **The board tools (`com.submit`, notes, questions) carry no language hint of their own.** Whoami's second rule covers board messages, notes and questions by name; the four descriptions that do name the fallback are the ones whose text lands on the home.
- **The full tier was not run.** No disposable stack exists and starting one would not fit the free disk; the two assertions iteration 2 changed were checked by reading the whoami path and by running `buildProtocol` with the candidate's module.
