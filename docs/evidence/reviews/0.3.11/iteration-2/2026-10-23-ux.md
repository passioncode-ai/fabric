## Iteration 1's fixes, checked

UX-level rows (UX sources, and rows whose fix is a thing a person sees):

- **V1-5 (UX-1), holds.** «Агенты в работе» is one line, 175 px wide (ru) and 159 px (en), 22 px tall, at 1440 to 900 px; its two buttons wrap under it (`layout.log`, `home-ru-1280.png`). The fix's `white-space:nowrap` on `.lh-agents .lp-panel-head h3` also reaches the project page, see UX-3.
- **V1-6 (UX-2), holds at the default width.** A project's agent is one line, 37 px, with an ellipsis, at every width (`project-ru-1280.png`). At 1150 px and below the line keeps 30 px for the task in ru («Работает н…»), see UX-3.
