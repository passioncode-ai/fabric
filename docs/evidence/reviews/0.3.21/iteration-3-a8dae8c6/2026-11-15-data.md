## Checked and sound
- **DA-13 fixed.**
  - `pathTails` (`boardSearch.ts:99-103`) keeps at most `PATH_TAILS` (8) tails, longest first. It is the only source of tails on both sides (`:117`, `:170`).
  - A tail always starts at a word, since a run holds exactly one separator between words (`:122-123`).
  - The worst case after the bound is a run with 8 slashes at its start followed by a long `-`-joined run. It gives 53 883 term characters for a 6 000-character run, at most 9 times what is read, so the cost is linear.
  - Spec §3a's «linear in what it reads» and «a path gives at most 8 tails» therefore hold.
  - Test «DA-13 (run three)» would fail on run three's code: 2 999 slashed tails against a limit of 9.
- **ER-20 fixed as stated.**
  - A query path searches only its tails that still hold a separator, so `docs/board` does not search `board` (test «ER-20 (run three)», `probe2.mjs`).
  - The query stays bounded at 300 characters and 12 words (`workTools.ts:404`, `QUERY_WORDS`), so at most about 9 terms per word. The probe above shows no step change in cost.
  - Each query word is still counted once in `matched`, `searched` and `not_searched`.
- **ER-21 fixed.** `GENERIC_NAMES` gains the per-folder names and `touchWords` drops a leading `+` (`:298-300`, `:309`). Spec §1 calls the list a heuristic. `similar_work` still reads only this project's live claims.
- **UX-18 / ER-22 fixed.**
  - Example: `days: 1`, a day that does not fit, then a failed 6-hour read (`boardSearchRead.ts:126`). The asked window is kept but capped.
  - `board_narrowed` now says «of the last 1 day(s) only the oldest messages were read: the newest were not; another window could not be read» (`:281`).
  - The note drops «or a wider days» whenever the board is capped (`:292`).
  - The two `board_narrowed` lines never both apply (`>= days` against `< days`), and the ladder never returns a window wider than asked (`:109`).
  - The ladder still costs at most 15 `com.list` pages plus 5 directory pages.
- **UX-19 at the data level.**
