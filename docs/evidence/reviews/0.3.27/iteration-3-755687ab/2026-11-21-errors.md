**Run five's errors fixes, checked by probe:**
- **ER-46 holds while the queue is not wedged.** A stopped CEO with a line waiting, and an ended one, are started by a new send. Both lines are typed in order and a second console opens (P6a, P6b). ER-53 breaks this when the drain timer fired while the CEO was stopped (P15).
- **ER-47 holds.** A line the queue typed is owed to its console. A limit in the answer retypes it once on Hermes (run-five test; P1).
- **ER-48 holds for sends that overlap.** Two sends to a CEO that has not started are typed `first,second`, each once, with one console opened (P5). ER-55 covers a different path where order still breaks.
- **ER-49 holds.** A limit seen while a send waits: the write into the new console is unknown, the answer is `delivery-unknown`, and the line is not queued again (P9).
- **ER-50 holds.** After five misses, the person's retry types the waiting line at once (P7). ER-59 covers what is left.
- **ER-51 holds.** A send during a files line's settled-Enter wait is typed after the Enter. Sequence: `see…`, `raw:"\r"`, `NEXT` (P8).
- **ER-52 holds.** `index.ts#runVoiceTool` answers `delivery-unknown` with «do not ask again; read ceo_reply» and starts the echo window.
- **No spin and no hang.** The drain loop awaits a delivery on every pass. A console that fails at once is tried once per drain (P10). `deps.deliver` never throws and always resolves by the delivery queue's deadline. `fallBack` and `start` catch their own failures, so `await pendingFallback` and `await ensure()` in `send` cannot reject or hang.
- **DA-40 holds.** A full queue refuses before any attachment is saved (run-five test).

**Blocking:**
- **ER-53.** Once `drain()` runs while the CEO is not running and a line waits, the queue stops for good. `draining` keeps a settled promise that is never cleared. From then on no message is typed until Fabric quits, and every new message is said as «CEO занят … уйдёт само». The usual trigger is a restart of Fabric with a line waiting: the 10 s timer fires before the person opens the CEO.
