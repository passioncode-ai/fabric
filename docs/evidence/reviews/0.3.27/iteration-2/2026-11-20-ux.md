## Verdict
**Not ready. 1 blocking finding, 9 non-blocking.**

What works:
- I verified these iteration-1 ux fixes by running the real `createCeoOperations` and `findProject` (probes kept in the scratchpad, never in the checkout):
  - **UX-1 holds.** «SEO», «temple», «Диззи» and «Саунд энджин» all come back with `understood_as` and `choices`, and nothing starts. Calling again with the exact name starts the session. Once an alias is kept, «Диззи» starts in one call.
  - **UX-2 holds for absolute folders.** A subfolder (`…/fabric/apps`) and a worktree Fabric knows are both refused with where to go.
  - **UX-3 holds.** The choice `research-agent (<folder>)` starts the right project.
  - **UX-6 holds.** By voice the start waits 8 s, then answers `pending` and points at `session_task`.
  - **UX-8 holds.** The `next` step offers `session_open`, and the instructions state the limits in both languages.
  - **UX-9 holds.** A name can be forgotten, and another project's name is refused as an alias.
  - **UX-13 holds.** `~/DATA/fabric` starts, and «~» alone is refused as too broad.
  - **UX-5 is partly fixed.** «Банк» no longer gets far `near` names. The new last-words match is what causes UX-15.
  - **UX-10 holds.** `written_unconfirmed` comes with its `next` step in the start answer.
  - **UX-11 and UX-12 hold.** I read them in the code and in the SCN-183 amendment.
- The voice instructions, Russian and English, say the same thing and contain no em dash. The only em dashes in the added lines are region markers.
- Tests on the candidate all pass:

- The three realistic flows each take two or three tool calls and one question to the owner:
  1. Start work in a project named by ear: `session_projects` or `session_start`, then ask the owner, then `session_start` with the exact name, then `session_task`.
  2. Answer a session's question: `session_read`, then `session_input`.
  3. Ask the CEO and hear its answer: `ask_ceo`, then `ceo_reply`.

Blocking:
- **UX-15.** If the owner names an agent's worktree by its folder name or its last words («digest runner», «review», «runner»), a session starts at once in the project's main folder. Nothing is asked back. The match also overrides a real project that the words or the sound would have found. 0.3.26 refused these names.
