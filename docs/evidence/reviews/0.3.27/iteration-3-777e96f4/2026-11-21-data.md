# Fabric 0.3.27 release review, iteration 3: data
Level data, candidate 777e96f49c90770f25c21803d46b34c6074ff922, baseline 44cd80ca, reviewer Claude Opus 5.5, read-only. HEAD before/after 777e96f4/777e96f4, dirty files before/after 0/0.

## Verdict
**Not ready. 1 blocking finding, 6 non-blocking.**

What works:
- **DA-46 holds.**
  - The loader now keeps up to `CEO_QUEUE_MAX + KEPT_LINES` (60) lines.
  - A file past 60 is kept aside whole (`over-bound`) before it is cut, then written back.
  - `enqueue` and `send` count only the person's own lines (`personLines`) against 50.
  - AGENTS.md:189, CHANGELOG.md:39 and CHANGELOG.md:145 now give the real bound: 50 of the person's lines, plus up to 10 that a lost console owed.
  - Test «a fallback on a full queue, then a restart of Fabric, keeps every line» passes.
- **DA-47 holds.**
  - A name dated more than a day in the future counts as old.
  - The file just kept aside is never removed.
  - Only plain files count toward the three.
  - The test «names dated in the future never evict the file just kept aside» passes.
- **DA-48 holds.** `SentLine` carries `at`, and `fallBack` puts `e.at ?? now()` into the requeued line. `sent.push` happens at one place only (`ceoAgent.ts:276`), and it passes `head.at`.
- **The person's stop drops owed lines.** `stopNow` removes the lines a lost console owed and keeps the person's own waiting lines. It records `ceo.agent.requeued-dropped` as a count only.
- **The voice's board reads (`voiceBoard.ts`) only read.**
  - Every read is bounded: projects 1 000, tasks 200, `linksTo` 200, chunked `selectIn`.
  - Every answer goes through `redactPayload` before it reaches xAI and the call journal (`index.ts`, `ceoAgentVoiceTool`).
  - The host logs only the tool's name (`ceo.voice.board`).
- **`ceo-window.json` holds four numbers.** It is written to a temp file and renamed. A broken file opens the window at its default.
- **The console line only reads, and keeps nothing on disk.**
  - Its Switchboard calls are limited to the read-only list (`readOnlySwitchboardCall`).
  - They are cached for 30 s and 120 s, and the folder cache is capped at 200.
  - It reads 64 KB of the transcript's tail for the model name.
  - The account label stays in memory and in the window.
  - The ids are copied without being shown.
- **Tests pass:**
  - `ceo-agent`: 79 of 79
  - `voice-retro`: 40 of 40
  - `voice-board`: 6 of 6
  - `estate-index`: 11 of 11
  - `memory-read`: 7 of 7
  - `delivery-queue`: 11 of 11
  - `claude-transcript`: 3 of 3
  - `inbox-auto-connect`: 2 of 2
  - `console-open`: 14 of 14
  - vitest on `sessionMeta`, `windowPlacement`, `SessionMetaLine`, `estateSearch` and `voiceCall`: 51 of 51

Blocking:
- DA-49: the CEO's knowledge door gives the CEO's model other sessions' transcripts, board messages and mail without redaction. A secret typed into any session on the Mac comes back word for word. This breaks the 0.3.26 rule that another session's turns are redacted before they reach a model. It also makes the CHANGELOG's «nothing leaves the machine» false.
