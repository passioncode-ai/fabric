**Run nine's ux fixes hold, with two small parts left.**

That is ruled into CO-620 (5) and is not claimed anywhere.
- **UX-75 holds.** A recalled transcript with a task opens a new console in its project with that task (`ceoTools.ts#resume`). A task headed for Switchboard is refused, not dropped. A refusal for a found session says Fabric found it.
- **UX-76 holds for the scope.** The rule now applies only to a word that stands alone as an address. The English duplicate is left (UX-86).
- **UX-77 holds.** A project whose own name matches a catalogue name exactly is searched as that project (`knowledgeTools.ts#search`).

- **UX-79 holds.** `ceoVoice.ts#voiceInstructions` says ids are for the tools, in both languages.
- **UX-80 holds in the part that was fixed.** A restore's removal no longer counts as a no (`board.ts` `saidNo`). Fabric's own late withdrawal is ruled into CO-620 (7).

**Blocking, one line each.**
- **UX-81:** the person's stop of the CEO lives only in memory. After any restart of Fabric (installing 0.3.27 included), a waiting `ceo.task` request starts the stopped CEO with no window and no word. That makes «a CEO the person stopped stays stopped» (CHANGELOG, ADR-0197 Amendment 5, AGENTS.md) false, and SCN-187's «a restart of Fabric starts the CEO with its window, as before».

| UX-81 | Blocking | **A CEO the person stopped is started again by a board request after Fabric restarts.** The hold is `let held = false` in memory, and `ceo.json` keeps only the project id. So `ceoBoardKeeper.wake` calls `ensure`, which starts the console on Opus and types the PM's request into it. The owner stopped it on purpose (the owner, 2026-11-19, SCN-187). He opened no window and is told nothing. Before this branch, after a restart the CEO started only when the person opened its window. The common restart is the 0.3.27 update itself, or «Перезапустить Fabric». Three claims are now false. The CHANGELOG bullet «The CEO is reachable on the board…» and ADR-0197 Amendment 5 §2 say «a CEO the person stopped stays stopped», with no scope. SCN-187's expected result says «a restart of Fabric starts the CEO with its window, as before». AGENTS.md's «The CEO console» row still names only the person under «Started by», and says the CEO «starts nothing until the person retries or restarts it». | `main/ceoAgent.ts:121` (`let held = false`), `:358` (`ceo.json` holds `{ projectId }` only), `#knownProjectId`. `main/ceoBoardKeeper.ts#wake`. `main/index.ts` `ceoBoardTick`. `docs/ux/scenarios.md` SCN-187 «Expected result». | UX-82 | Non-blocking | **The wake is said to no one, and its log line says «woken» when nothing started.** A wake opens no window, writes no act note and no feed line, so the owner finds an Opus CEO running with no reason shown. `wake` records `ceo.board-wake ok` before `ensure`.
