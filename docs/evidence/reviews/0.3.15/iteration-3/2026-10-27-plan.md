### PL-8 (non-blocking): the Release section is incomplete against the runbook and 0.3.14's precedent

Evidence. `docs/evidence/plans/2026-10-27-release-0315-verification.md` «Release» says: the release commit raises `package.json`, dates the CHANGELOG, writes `release-gate.json`; `bash scripts/ci.sh fast` and the gate run; the tag `v0.3.15` is pushed after they read green and `release.yml` is watched to its published assets; then the rollout. That agrees with `release-mac.md` steps 1 to 6 (version bump, finalized changelog, gate, `verifiedCommit` ancestor, tag, `release.yml` through `publish`). What it leaves out, each of which 0.3.14's ledger carried («Hosted CI on the final candidate», «Owed in 0.3.14's Release close») and the runbook names:

1. Hosted full-tier CI on the verified commit (the full tier needs a database this Mac cannot hold; the guard job also reads `ci.yml` on the tagged commit, release-mac.md step 4). The ledger does not say which run is read or when.
2. Post-tag steps: `node scripts/workspace.mjs publish` and `check --require-child` (runbook step 10), the knowledge-base update (step 11), the smoke of the published DMG (step 7), and the board posts.
3. Which files may change after `verifiedCommit`. The runbook lists only `CHANGELOG.md`, `release-gate.json`, the ledger, `docs/MERGES.md`, `docs/reports/map.html`, declared review reports and receipts, gate-listed handoffs and `package.json` version. `docs/evidence/backlog.md` (P-30 to «released», the Now line), the carry-over register and the scenarios are not on it, so they must land after the tag (0.3.14 did exactly this: «filed as carry-over rows on main after the tag, since the register cannot change after verifiedCommit»). The 0.3.15 ledger does not say so, and a release commit that also edits P-30 would fail `verifiedCandidateProblem`.
4. Iteration 3's own section and receipts are the one place the ledger and the three-section rule meet: the section must name `dbb69aa4` in full and end with the `Exit for iteration 3` line, and the gate's iteration 3 `candidateCommit` must equal `verifiedCommit`. The ledger says «iteration 3's candidate ... becomes the verified commit» but does not say that any iteration 3 fix (a new candidate) re-opens the iteration, which the runbook implies.

Fix. Add to «Release» a short ordered list: hosted full-tier run read before the tag (run id recorded in the ledger, as 0.3.14 did); after the tag: workspace publish and `check --require-child`, knowledge-base row, DMG smoke, P-30 «released» and the Now line, carry-over edits, board post. State that these register edits land after the tag because they are outside the post-`verifiedCommit` allowlist. Non-blocking: the plan is consistent with the runbook where it speaks, and the omissions are the follow-up the maintainer already performs; they should be written down before the release commit, not after.
