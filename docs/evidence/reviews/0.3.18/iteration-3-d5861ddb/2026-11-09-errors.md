| ER-27 | non-blocking (low) | ER-25's residue, moved from the request's age to the workflow's age. A request whose board deadline is more than seven days out keeps one workflow across all its runs (`prior.workflowId`, taken back each time), and that workflow's frozen packet deadline is the opening write plus seven days less a minute. When the request is still dispatched after that (a streak of Switchboard refusals, which do not count as empty runs, backing off up to 6 h; or deferrals), the take-back is accepted and the run starts, but its `dispatch` view reads `deadline` in the past and `keys: closed, deadline-passed` while the board's deadline (which `candidate` and `runEndsAt` follow) is days ahead. A run may read that as an expired request and end without answering, an empty run; two send the request to the operator. No key is lost (the packet names no `credentials`). Not a regression: 0.3.17 could not open such a workflow at all, and 0e501425 reached the same state sooner. | `boardDispatchRun.ts` `open` (take-back of `prior.workflowId`, `body(false)`); `boardDispatch.ts#packetDeadline`; `dispatch_packet.py#_shut` (`now >= deadline`), `#view`; `_shut` probe at `+7d` | Before taking a workflow back, compare its frozen deadline (keep the written value in the `DispatchRecord`, or read `dispatch.deadline` from `observatory_checkpoint_latest`) with the board's; when it has passed or is within `LAUNCH_MARGIN_MS` while the board's is ahead, close the old workflow and open a new one (`-open`, `body(true)`).
