**NOT_RUN**

- The installed app (the brief forbids it). Electron 44's Chromium is represented by Chrome; the layout is the real React tree with the real stylesheets, but on a stand-in bridge, so pages show their failed-read states and real content heights will differ.
- Three places could not be measured on the stand-in bridge: «Create an agent», «Open a project from one folder» and «Bring in an agent» render the app's crash screen ("Something in this window failed") because a call the bridge does not serve throws inside render; the Fabric chat from Help does the same; the private history panel once ran Chrome out of memory on the stand-in. These are artefacts of `mock.js`, not findings. A console needs a running terminal session and was not measured on the real tree (the shipped browser test's console cases pass).
- «Where work is» (`active.kind === 'where'`) has no entry point in the renderer (`grep "kind: 'where'"` finds only its render branch in `App.tsx`), so a person cannot reach it.

## Not findings

- **Every other place scrolls on the real tree.** Home, Agents, History, Settings (and its «Ещё» places: Account limits, What projects tell each other, Fabric's name and look, Help), the board, the plan, the pulse, the first run, the workspace, «＋ Create», the scan and «Create a project»: wherever the content is taller than the page, the wheel reaches its end and `scrollTop` reaches the bottom, at all seven sizes (`matrix.mjs`, `wheel: end`, `direct: true`); the rail, the top bar, the notice and the act line do not move (`pinned: true`); `docScroll` 0. V1-1 (Settings) holds: 1085 px of Settings in a 558 px page at 1280x720 reaches its end, and so does 1430 px in 198 px at 640x360.
- **No clipping at the side, no overlap with the act line.** Window, place and page show no sideways overflow at any width from 1440 to 640 px, in en and ru (`matrix.mjs` `side: 0`; `ru.mjs` `wide: []`); the page's bottom equals the act line's top in every row (for example 678 and 678 at 1280x720, 318 and 318 at 640x360), so nothing scrolls under the act line.
- **The folded rail** below 900 px is 64 px and every rail place is reached by its `title` (`ru.mjs` and `matrix.mjs` at 800 and 640 px).
- **Short windows and 200 % text**: 1280x600 and 640x360 reach the foot of every measured page; at 640x360 the page keeps 198 to 240 px.
- **Notices**: with three long banners the page keeps exactly half the place (338 of 676, 158 of 316) and the act line stays at the window's foot; each banner scrolls inside itself (V1-3 holds). At 640x360 that leaves each banner 20 to 30 px tall, a slit a person scrolls through; three notices at 200 % text in a 360 px window is an edge case and the design states it.
- **Side panels** sit beside the page, end at the act line and scroll by themselves; the wheel over a panel never scrolls the page (`panels.mjs`); the 1440 px agents grid rule holds (`grid.mjs`: middle column the widest from 1441 px).
- **A new place starts at its top** (V1-8 holds on the real tree): another rail place, another project, the workspace from a project, Settings and back (`reset.mjs`). The keyboard path after a rail move is ruled to CO-384 and recorded in SCN-162's Scroll line; I did not re-file it.
- **Strings**: the release adds no i18n key (`git diff --stat v0.3.9..HEAD -- …/i18n` empty), `check-design.mjs` passes, the ru titles render (`Главная`, `Настройки`, `История`, `Агенты`), and no added line in `CHANGELOG.md` or `docs/ux` carries U+2014.
- **The shipped tests** pass (77 browser checks, 19 unit tests); the browser suite runs in the local gate (`scripts/ci.sh:519`) and says NOT_RUN where Playwright and Chrome are absent (V1-5).
