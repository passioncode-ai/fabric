## Not findings

- **Iteration 2's blocking UX-1 is fixed on the real tree.** The window never scrolls in any of the 280 place x size x locale rows: the wheel over the rail and the top bar, a click on the rail followed by PageDown, End, Space and ArrowDown, and the wheel past the page's end leave `document.scrollingElement.scrollTop` at 0 and the rail, the top bar and the act line where they were; the document never overflows (`docMax` 0), on the project page included. A screenshot of the ru project page after 10 wheel turns over the rail and 30 over the page shows the rail, the top bar and the notice in place and the page at 3748 (`ux3/shots/project-ru-after-wheel.png`). The two rules are defence in depth: each alone keeps the browser case green, both removed fail it.
- **`position: relative` on the pages and panels moves nothing else.** The only absolutely placed things in the renderer are the two visually hidden label rules (now placed against their page), `.lp-feed-dot` and `.lp-timeline article:before` (their articles are `position: relative`), and `.lh-chart-tip` (its button is `position: relative`); `.fabric-launcher` is `fixed` and unaffected; no renderer code reads `offsetTop`, `offsetParent` or scrolls the window. Monaco lives only in the detached `EditorWindow`, which has no `.launch-root`.
- **Every page reaches its foot, by wheel and by Tab,** in en and ru, at 1440, 1280, 1024, 800 and 640 px wide, in short windows (1280x600, 640x360) and at 200 % text (640x360): nothing is clipped at the side, nothing of the page stays under the act line, and every Tab stop is in view. The places iteration 2 could not measure («Open a project from one folder», «Create an agent», «Bring in an agent», the Fabric chat) now render and pass.
- **Returning from Settings keeps the page** (V2-9): Chrome keeps the hidden page's scroll position, and the scroll key does not include Settings. SCR-84's "closing Settings finds the page under it where it was" is true.
- **A new page of the place starts at its top** (another place, another project, the workspace); opening a board item, a task or an entity inside the same page does not reset it (`scrollKeyOf` takes `item` only from the route, and BoardScreen selects rows in its own state).
- **Print shows the whole page** without the rail, the top bar or the act line (6-page PDFs for home and the project page).
- **The agents grid** takes one column beside a panel up to 1440 px and three from 1441, as the notes now say.
- **Notices with one banner** leave the page half the place and scroll inside themselves; the act line stays at the window's foot (`squeeze.mjs`, 1280x720 and 1280x400).
- **Strings and text.** The release adds no i18n key; `check-design.mjs` passes with ru covering every key; no added line in `CHANGELOG.md`, `docs/ux` or the strings carries U+2014. The em dashes in added lines elsewhere are old text rewritten in place (`docs/evidence/backlog.md` "Now" line, historical) or a placeholder field (`docs/MERGES.md`), which the docs level owns.
- **SCN-162 and SCR-84 tell the truth** about the behaviour measured here, apart from UX-2's proof gap. The notes do not mention print or the return from Settings; they claim nothing false about them.
- **Light theme** was not measured; the scroll rules carry no colour.
