## Not findings

- **`revealSection` and the `scrollIntoView` callers now that `main.content` is the scroller.** `revealSection` walks to the nearest ancestor with `overflow-y: auto|scroll` and content to scroll, which is `.content` (probe case D: box `content`, scrollTop 1452, `.fab-place` 0, top bar and act line unchanged). On a page shorter than the place it falls back to `scrollIntoView`, which moved nothing (case D2). `scrollIntoView` near the end of a long page (`ProjectHome.tsx:340`, `EstateHome.tsx:589`, `evidence.ts` `reveal`) scrolled only `.content` (case D, scrollTop 3050). The only callers that can reach `.fab-place` are those whose target is outside `.content`, which today is Settings (ER-2).
- **`revealSection` after a project change.** `App.tsx:1082`, `1092`, `1151` call `openProject` then `revealSection`. When the `placeKey` effect's reset to the top lands after `revealSection`'s first alignment, the align loop (`requestAnimationFrame(step)` until `settleMs` 1500 ms, `LaunchShell.tsx:65` to `67`) aligns again on the next frame, so the section still ends in view.
- **The `placeKey` effect with no `.shell`, or several.** No `.shell`: only `.fab-place.scrollTo` runs and nothing throws (`box.scrollTo?.`). Several: `querySelectorAll` resets each. The `:scope > .shell > .content` selector matches App's markup (`.shell` is a direct child of `.fab-place` through `LaunchShell`'s children, `App.tsx:977`; `main.content` a direct child of `.shell`, `App.tsx:1002`). `ProjectHome.tsx:181` uses the same selector.
- **`placeKey` granularity.** Board, pulse, plan, releases, guide and comms share one key per kind (`App.tsx:773`), so a change of project within one kind would keep the scroll offset; I found no path that moves between two of them without passing another place (`App.tsx:1119`, `1132`, `1143`, `1200`, `1254`; `openEntity` goes through `openProject`, `App.tsx:636`). Recorded, not a defect.
- **The embedded console and xterm fit.** `.session-window{height:100%}` resolves against the bounded `.content`: the terminal is 1064 x 578 inside a 1064 x 634 page with no scroll (case F); 16 px below it is `.content`'s new `padding-bottom` (`rail.css:62`), smaller than 0.3.9's `calc(2.5rem + var(--space-3) * 2)` (`styles.css:126`).
- **A single notice taller than a third.** Capped at 223 of 676 px and scrolls inside itself; the page and the act line keep their place (case B2).
- **Very short window.** At 300 px tall the page keeps 161 px between a one-line notice and the act line (case C). The 800 px width cases of the shipped suite pass.
- **The act line absent.** `ActLine` returns `null` without an act (`launch/ActLine.tsx`, `if (!act) return null`); `.shell{flex:1 1 0}` then takes the whole place. The scan's sticky `.st-foot` (`start/start.css:68`) sticks to `.content`'s foot above the act line (shipped suite, `scan` cases).
- **Detached windows.** `EditorWindow` and `SessionWindow` are returned before the shell (`App.tsx:886`, `887`); every rule in `rail.css` is scoped to `.fab-*`, `.rail` or `.act-line`, so they are unaffected.
- **Side panels.** All four render `aside.ceo-panel`, which has its own `overflow-y: auto` (`styles.css:314` to `318`) and `min-height:0` in the shell (`rail.css:63`); the shipped suite's `panel` cases pin them.
- **`scrollbar-gutter: stable`.** On classic scrollbars (Windows, Linux, macOS "always show") it reserves a strip beside every page, the console included; this Chrome draws overlay scrollbars, so I could not measure it (`probe-bars.mjs`: `clientWidth` = `offsetWidth`). Cosmetic, not an error.
- **The dead `.fab-place>.app-shell-content` selector** (`rail.css:60`): no markup carries `app-shell-content` (`grep -rn app-shell-content apps/desktop/src/renderer/src` finds only the CSS), so its loss of specificity to the notice rule at `rail.css:65` has no effect.
- **Test hygiene.** `Rail.test.tsx` restores `HTMLElement.prototype.scrollTo` in `finally` and unstubs globals in `afterEach` (`Rail.test.tsx:12`); `vitest.config.ts` sets `restoreMocks: true`.
