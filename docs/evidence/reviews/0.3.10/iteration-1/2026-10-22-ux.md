## Not findings

- **Every other place scrolls.** `App.tsx:1002` to `:1266` renders console, history, board, comms, quota, help, welcome, start, guide, persona, pulse, releases, plan, where, agents, home, draft (Onboarding), the project page and its workspace inside `main.content`, the one scroller (`launch/rail.css:62`); the shipped browser test passes its 54 checks (exit 0), and my probes reach the last row of a synthetic page at 1280 and 800 px.
- **The rail, the top bar, the side panel and the act line stay put** while the page scrolls (shipped test, all five pinned elements equal before and after 60 wheel turns, both widths); a side panel scrolls on its own and leaves the page where it was (`probe.mjs`: last hit at 652 above the act line, page 0).
- **No sideways scroll and no clipping at the side.** Window, place and page scroll widths stay within their client widths at 1280, 800 and 640 px (shipped test and `probe.mjs`, `sideways:0`).
- **Focus after scroll.** Tabbing through 30 buttons of a long page brought each into view above the act line (`tabbedButtonsOutOfView: []`); the top bar is a flex item now, not a sticky overlay, so nothing covers the top of the page. `revealSection` re-aligns every animation frame for 1.5 s (`LaunchShell.tsx:50` to `:66`), so the place effect's scroll to the top cannot cancel a reveal that follows `openProject`.
- **The folded rail under 900 px** keeps 64 px and the page fills the rest (`probe.mjs`, `railWidth:64` at 640 px); the 900 px media rule is unchanged (`rail.css:67`).
- **200 % text without notices**: the page keeps 274 of 316 px at 640 x 360 and reaches its end (`dbg.mjs`: scrollTop 3993, last row above the act line).
- **The console fills its page**: right edge flush, a 16 px band under it (the page's bottom padding, smaller than the 64 px (2.5rem plus two 12 px steps) `styles.css:124` gave before), no scroll (`console.mjs`).
- **The scan's «Add N as projects» stays above the act line** (shipped test, `scan` fixture, both widths); `.st-foot` is the only sticky element left (`start/start.css:68`).
- **Strings.** The release adds no i18n key, so ru and en parity is unchanged (`check-design.mjs`: 2517 of 2517 keys in ru, no monolingual additions). No U+2014 in any added line of `CHANGELOG.md`, `docs/ux` or the i18n files. The CHANGELOG entry names the operator's words and the controls the person sees.
- **Stylesheet order.** The bundle loads `rail.css` before `styles.css` (App's subtree first); the new rules win by specificity (`.fab-place>.shell>.content` 0,3,0 over `.content` 0,1,0; `.fab-place>.shell` over `.shell`), and every probe gave identical output in both orders.
- **Print.** `rail.css:75` lets the shell, the place and the page grow in print; not measured beyond reading.
- **The agents grid under 1250 px beside a panel** is one column that ends at the panel's left edge with no sideways scroll (`probe.mjs` at 1240 px); the release's claim holds. UX-5 is the band just above it.
