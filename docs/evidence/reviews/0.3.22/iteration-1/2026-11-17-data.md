## Checked and sound
- No migration in 0.3.22; no new journal event type, so no `event_types` row or feed sentence is owed. New payload keys (`work.released@1` `wait`, `work.claimed@1` `workflow_id` and `resumed_from`) are ignored by the projectors (`apply_work_claims`, `20261011000095_work_claims.sql:107-175`) and pass the legacy ingress roster (`desktopIngress.ts:9-13`).
- A claim handed back at enrollment stands only where the lease projector made the new session the owner (`20261011000095_work_claims.sql:131-137`), and is read back before the task is moved (`main/resumedClaims.ts:93-97`); its idempotency key is unique per session and old claim (`work_claims_key`); its renewal is registered (`index.ts:1415`, `agentSurface.ts#noteClaims`).
- The remote question is idempotent by `asked_command_id` (unique index, `20260908000036_question_ask_idempotency.sql:16-18`), read back by id, and its states (`open`, `answered`, `withdrawn`, `stale`) are exactly the table's check (`20260906000028_questions.sql:36`).
- The `waiting` record is validated, persisted and kept while its request is read on the board (`boardDispatchRun.ts:144-147`, `:1327-1328`); it is not counted as an empty run; 0.3.22 reads every 0.3.21 record (all new fields optional), and 0.3.21 keeps resume records (probe).
- `turnVerdictRead.ts` is bounded (20 claims, 1 heartbeat by primary key, 3 turns by the indexed `payload->>session_id`) and every failure gives no verdict with an ops line.
- Switchboard holds follow the claims: set on claim, renewal and release, released at the session's end and when renewal finds none (`agentSurface.ts:600`, `:1097-1102`, `:1121-1143`); a quit keeps them until the claim's expiry, at most 7 days by `HOLD_MAX_MS`.
- `bugs.json` is bounded (200 rows, 30 days, 0600, atomic rename, `bugStore.ts:10-12`, `:83-92`); `people-asks.json` is bounded (2000 records, a month for closed ones, `peopleAskRun.ts:29-30`, `:380-383`).
- The settings keys `resumeLeftWork` and `peopleAsk` default to on, are window-patchable, and validated (`shared/appSettings.ts:40`, `:169`, `:176`).
- The moved board, task, effect, project and question tools add no unbounded `.in(` (`grep` on the new modules: none).
