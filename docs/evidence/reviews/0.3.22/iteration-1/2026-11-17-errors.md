## Checked and sound

- `readTurnVerdict`: every read is bounded by rows (`limit`), a refused or thrown read is logged as
  `work.turn-verdict-unread` and gives no verdict, never a failed turn (`turnVerdictRead.ts:35-36`, `:54-63`); the
  turn is appended before the verdict is read, so the newest turn is this one. The journal read is served by
  `journal_by_session_id` (migration `20261020000116`); `session_heartbeats` is keyed by `session_id`, so `limit(1)`
  is the row.
- `turnVerdict`: a declared wait, no open live claim, or two turns that changed nothing give no verdict; unknown facts
  never count as a change, so an unreadable folder cannot keep a session going (`turnVerdict.ts:22-27`, `:34-41`).
- `sessionHolds` child: no shell, `timeout` 10 s and a bounded buffer; a timeout or spawn error rejects (non-numeric
  `code`) and is logged through `failed` as `session.hold` (`index.ts:1329-1330`); a non-zero exit is logged with its
  first 300 characters of stderr and not remembered, so the next change asks again; a bad session id is never passed.
- `sessionEnded`: the hold is released only for a session that really ended, not on Fabric quitting
  (`agentSurface.ts:598-601`).
- `leave`: the decision is synchronous, so two clients leaving at once end the session exactly once (the first drops
  itself, the second finds no other live client); covered by the two fb5b790a tests.
- `fabric_work_release` with `wait`: refused with `done`, refused when it names nothing checkable, journalled in
  `work.released@1` and read back by claim id (`workTools.ts:644-646`, `boardResumeRead.ts:29-40`).
- `boardResumeRead`: every main read that fails throws, and the dispatcher logs it as `board.dispatch.resume-read`
  failed and goes on with requests and inboxes (`boardDispatchWiring.ts:545`); a capped read and unreadable
  Switchboard limit stops are each said once per change.
- Resume starts: a Switchboard without `--reason resume` is refused locally (`resume_unsupported`), said once, and
  waits on the dispatcher's exponential back-off (10 minutes up to 6 hours), so it cannot spin.
- T5: a remote question is filed under a deterministic `asked_command_id` (projected from `command_id`, migration
  `20260908000036`), so a lost append answer is found on the next pass rather than asked twice; a refusal is retried
  with the same key at most `CLOSE_ATTEMPTS` times, an unclear one ends the run as before with its reason logged; an
  unread board files nothing; a request settled before filing files nothing; the `waiting` record does not count as an
  empty run and its workflow is closed by the remote close loop. The question's texts are scrubbed by
  `cleanOriginalText`, which redacts credentials (probe above).
- `remoteQuestionOf`: a fenced block regex anchored per line with a lazy body, `JSON.parse` in a try, shape checked
  before `normaliseAsk`; a body without the block reads as before.
- The disk floor holds new starts only and keeps watching runs in flight; the crossing is said once.
- `task-to-operator` is in `ABANDONED_KINDS` and in the `requests` source's kinds, so a failed requests read keeps its
  last telling; its subject and «На проверке» words exist in both locales.
- Enrollment waits for claims given back at most `RESUME_CLAIMS_WAIT_MS` (5 s, its timer cleared and unref'd) and logs
  a failure as `hub.session.resume-claims`; it never fails the enrollment.
- `fabric_question_ask` clarity check runs after the idempotency read, so a retry of a question already asked answers
  as before.
- The console: «Новая сессия» is disabled while it starts and says a failure under itself; «Завершить сессию» is the
  same `SessionStop` as before, with its refusal path unchanged.
