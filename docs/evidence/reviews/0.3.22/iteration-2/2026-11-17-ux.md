- Remote runs: the packet rule tells a run without a board how to end blocked with one fenced question; an answered or closed question opens the next run's packet with the person's words (`answerPacket`); an unclear one is named in the closing checkpoint.
- `task-to-operator`: the same meaning in ru and en, said in words, navigable to the task; the agent-side `nextStep` says Fabric starts no more runs until someone claims or moves the task.
- The console's one bar: «Завершить сессию» / "End session" with a tooltip of what stays (the same in ru and en), «Новая сессия» with its runner in the tooltip and its own failure line with the machine's words beneath; the shortened folder keeps the whole path on hover; the top bar alone names the session.
- Agent access: «Агент без имени» / "An unnamed agent" with the id said once below as where it registered; dates in words; a request leads with who and until when; «Что изменится после ответа» folds the rest.

- Comms: plural counts through `pluralForm` in both languages; «Все сообщения проектам прочитаны.» only when no project has unread messages or waiting requests.
- Settings: «Продолжать начатую работу без меня» is disabled while board starts are off and its help says the limits and when it waits for the person; «Присылать вопросы в Telegram через агента организации» says what goes, to whom and that it applies only where that agent takes such questions.
