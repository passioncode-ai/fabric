  0.3.21 reads that event only as a machine tick (`bfb4d31f:shared/workRhythm.ts:25`).
- The remote question: filed once per dispatch by `asked_command_id` (unique index `questions_one_per_command`,
  `20260908000036_question_ask_idempotency.sql:16-18`), its texts scrubbed by `cleanOriginalText`, read back by id with
  states that are the table's check (`20260906000028_questions.sql:36`), on the request's target project, `task_id` null.
- The `waiting` record: validated (`boardDispatchRun.ts:144-147`), written atomically with the other records, kept while
  its request is read on the board, not counted as an empty run, takes no concurrency slot (state `ended`), closes its
  workflow through the remote-ended path; the next run goes to the machine that asked and carries the answer cut at
  1500 characters inside 3990 of notes.
- Resume records: their own `resume` key, read back by `isDispatchRecord`; 0.3.22 reads every 0.3.21 record; the
  supervisor's reads are estate-scoped (`index.ts:1081`), bounded (500 releases, said `capped` once per change; chunks of
  100), and `readResumeState` reads one task and its newest claim by primary key and `work_claims_owner`/order.
- Claims hand-back: earlier sessions found through the indexed `journal_enrolments_by_runtime` and `journal_by_session_id`,
  limited to the same binding and project, each new claim read back from the projection before the task is moved; a
  failure never fails the enrollment (`sessionEnroll.ts`, at most `RESUME_CLAIMS_WAIT_MS`).
- Turn verdict: bounded (20 claims, the heartbeat by primary key `session_heartbeats.session_id`, 3 turns by
  `journal_by_session_id`); any failed read gives no verdict and an ops line.
- Switchboard holds: in memory only, released at the session's end and when no claim is left; after a Fabric restart a
  hold lasts at most until the claim expiry it was set to (`holdUntil`, at most 7 days by `HOLD_MAX_MS`).
- The second-client leave (fb5b790a) writes nothing new: a session not ended keeps its claims until they lapse or its
  last client leaves.
- Files: `people-asks.json` and `bugs.json` written 0600 by temp and rename (`index.ts:3322-3324`); `people-ask-routes.json`
  read only, a bad file falls back to the public route; personal projects match no route (`shared/peopleAsk.ts:283-288`).
