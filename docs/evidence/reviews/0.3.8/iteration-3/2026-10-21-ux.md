## On the decisions after iteration 2

- **`fabric.project.resolve` answers only a live `project.resolve` or `session.enroll` grant; the new lane refuses a disconnected agent:** right, and said consistently in the tool description, SCN-132, ADR-0166 and the notes; revoking in Settings now ends it. Only the binding-side words lag (UX-3).
- **Backoff (only no project grows to 10 min, failed to a minute, a 503 with Retry-After is a cooldown):** right for the measured case (a Hermes in the home folder) and for `failed`; too wide for the `no-project` refusals the person lifts in Fabric, which is the blocking UX-1. The 503 cooldown is right; its words are UX-4.
- **Refusal levels by code (CO-335):** log-only, no person or agent text changes; the notes describe it as the code does.
- **The decimal 3 GB line and its one number (SCN-161):** right; only the rounding edge (UX-2).
- **Session cap stays 8:** no user-facing text names 16 (`grep` of `CHANGELOG.md`, `scenarios.md`, i18n, `AGENTS.md`).
- **Wave 4 renumbered to 0.3.9:** holds in every text a person reads (История lede, Unreleased).
