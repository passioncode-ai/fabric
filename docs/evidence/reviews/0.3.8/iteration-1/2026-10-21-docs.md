## The patch's decisions

- **16 sessions per project.** The measured need was ten (a lead, five reviewers, four fixers); 16 leaves room for a
  second wave of subagents and keeps the 64 ceiling across projects, so a loop is still bounded. It is a reasonable
  bound. It does not change who is evicted at the cap: least recently active is still the waiting lead, so a larger
  team meets the same failure at 16. Its record is the missing ADR amendment (DO-6).
- **Typed refusals at warn with `error_code`.** Consistent with the trace contract: `refused` and `schema_error`
  already map to `ok`/warn in `OPS_OUTCOME`, `TOOL_OUTCOMES` is unchanged (`trace_outcome` stays `threw`), and
  `error_code` is the name the hub's own spans use. Its limits are untested and unstated (DO-9).
- **The 3 GB line.** Honest in what it claims ("Docker stops when the disk runs out", shown only for the three stack
  causes, nothing when the space cannot be read); the unit and the Russian number format need care (DO-10).
- **Wave 4 to 0.3.9.** Done in the app string, P-20, P-21, the Now line and 33 carry-over version cells, but not
  everywhere (DO-1 to DO-5).
