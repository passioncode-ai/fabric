## Does the patch match what the operator asked

The operator asked (2026-10-21): check everything done for 0.3.7, gather logs, errors, documents out of step, anything half done or misread, fix, release. The patch answers the log (202 notifications, typed refusals with their code), one error path (a full disk named), the session cap the release lead hit, the bare Windows pipe, and the renumbering. It misses three things the same audit should have found, all visible before the patch commit (01:26 UTC): Observatory's failed live check on the board (PL-2), the 0.3.7 release notes' own "in 0.3.8" promises (PL-3), and the half-done items of 0.3.7's Release close (PL-6, PL-7). It also leaves the docs gate red (PL-1).

Judgement on the decisions the brief names:

- **16 sessions per project** (`sessionEnroll.ts#MAX_ENROLLED_PER_BINDING`): a fair stopgap for the measured peak (a lead with nine subagents, under 16; the 64 ceiling across projects is unchanged and `session-enroll.test.mjs` covers per-project counting). It does not change the choice that ended the lead: least recently active picks the session that waits on its subagents, so a wider fan-out ends the lead again. Recorded nowhere as a decision (PL-8).
- **Typed refusals at warn with `error_code`** (`toolOutcome.ts#typedRefusalCode`, `toolTrace.ts`): right for the trace contract. `TOOL_OUTCOMES` is unchanged, `trace_outcome` stays `threw`, and the ops mapping `{ok, warn}` is the same one `refused` already has (`toolTrace.ts` `OPS_OUTCOME`). No repository reader of `trace_outcome` other than `agent-surface.test.mjs` exists, so CO-335's "decided with the trace's readers" holds by absence. The cause of the 148 refusals is not looked at (PL-9).
- **The 3 GB disk line** (`startupFailure.ts#LOW_DISK_BYTES`, `index.ts#explainAndQuit`): the threshold is sound against the evidence (OrbStack stopped under 1 GB; Inbox reported ENOSPC failures at 1 to 3 GB on thread `0abdfd6f`), it is shown only for the three stack causes and never when the space is unreadable, and the words are conditional. Two honesty limits, non-blocking (PL-10).
- **Renumbering to 0.3.9:** done in the carry-over ledger, P-20, the final pass (a dated note, the right form for a dated page), OF-13 and `acts.lede` in both languages. Not done in P-19, the plan README's own row, the onboarding plan's pointer, the handoff, the 0.3.7 release notes, or on the board (PL-1, PL-3, PL-4, PL-5).
