## On the three decisions

- **Per-OS disk line:** right, and now each OS's own words in both languages; its remaining faults are the unit of the threshold (UX-3), the floor (UX-4), the unnamed disk and the purgeable gap (UX-5) and the scenario that should say so (UX-2). 3 GB is a sensible trigger for a line that names a likely cause.
- **`project.resolve` access ask:** right in substance (read-only, of Fabric itself, immediate on the operator's own machine under ADR-0160, revocable); its words are not done: the person sees it as an unknown tool (UX-1) and no agent can discover how to ask (UX-7).
- **Growing `fabric-mcp` backoff:** right for the refusals nothing will change; too wide for `denied` and the folders the person can attach (UX-6).
