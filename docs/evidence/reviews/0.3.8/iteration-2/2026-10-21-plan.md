## Does the patch answer the operator's request

The operator asked on 2026-10-21 to check everything done for 0.3.7, gather logs, errors, documents out of step and anything half done or misread, fix it and release. At this candidate the patch answers the log (accepted notifications, typed refusals with their code), the error the machine hit (a full disk named at startup), the misread access shape that blocked Observatory (`project.resolve` admitted, answered on `617c8cbe` `e42909f5` at 06:21 UTC), the poller that caused most of the refusals (backoff to 30 min), the bare Windows pipe, and the renumbering, and it withdrew its one unsupported change (the cap of 16). What is still short is the "half done" half: a few renumbering lines, the cross-project asks that arrived during and after 0.3.7's final pass, the knowledge base's version rows, and the hosted full suite (PL-1, PL-4 to PL-8). None of those is code.

Judgement on the decisions the brief names:

- **16 sessions per project:** withdrawn, rightly. `sessionEnroll.ts:69` is `MAX_ENROLLED_PER_BINDING = 8`, unchanged since `v0.3.7` (no diff to the file); the ledger's audit section gives the reason (the lead's sessions ended on 0.3.6, whose cap counted every project, fixed by CO-334). Only `docs/MERGES.md:9` still lists `main/sessionEnroll.ts` (PL-9).
- **Typed refusals at warn with `error_code`:** right for the trace contract. `TOOL_OUTCOMES` is unchanged and the trace outcome stays `threw`; only the ops mapping changes (`toolTrace.ts`, `refusalOps`), and refusals that say Fabric or a product failed (`hub-unavailable`, `product-credential-unavailable`, `product-error`, `internal`) stay errors while `outcome-unknown` is `unknown` (`toolOutcome.ts:171` to `:178`). The record of it does not say so (PL-2).
- **The 3 GB disk line:** the threshold is sound for the evidence (OrbStack failed under 1 GB) and the dialog's number is honest (decimal GB, the reader's format, never below 0.1). Two gaps: the scenario does not say which volume is measured, though the ledger says it does (PL-2), and the threshold is 3 GiB while every claim says 3 GB (PL-3).
- **Renumbering to 0.3.9:** consistent in `CHANGELOG.md` (:33 to :34), История (`acts.lede` in both languages; `acts.empty` makes no promise), P-19, P-20's title, every open carry-over row's version column (CO-306 to CO-360 say 0.3.9), the simple plan's waves row, the final pass's dated note, OF-08's row, and on the board (the 06:21 UTC corrections on `bb87af23`, `8448237d`, `0f5d0589`). Not in the onboarding plan's banner, P-20's status, CO-291, and the 0.3.7 ledger's Release close (PL-1, PL-5).
