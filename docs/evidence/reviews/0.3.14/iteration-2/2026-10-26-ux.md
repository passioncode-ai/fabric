## Not findings

- The operator's must-haves of v2 (R-5, R-6, R-7 and the goal with progress, R-3, R-4) are built: the rail row opens the agent's screen, the console icon opens Fabric's console as a tab (`onAgentConsole('s1')`, probe S1), back, forward and home are in the top bar and disabled, never hidden, when there is nowhere to go (probe S1), and the step shows as a `progressbar` named «шаг 4 из 6» (probe H1).
- The left column's toggle is a labelled group with roving focus; arrow keys, Home and End move between the two views (probe S1, `LaunchShell.tsx#LaunchShell` `onViewKey`). The brand's accessible name is «Главная» with the waiting count (probe S1). Projects sort in the locale's collation; in Russian, Cyrillic names come before Latin ones (`Ёлка | atlas | Fabric | Nicegram`), which is what ICU's Russian collation does.
- Reading, failed and empty are said apart in the rail's agents view, the home's «В работе» and the strip; a number that was not read shows the dash placeholder with its reason, never 0 (probes S2, H2, H3).
- «Открыть сессию» opens the console only on press; an agent whose session runs outside Fabric says so in words, in the rail (a mark with a name) and on the screen (probes S1, A2).
- Removed places have a way in: the graph through a project page's «Связи» and the graph's own «whole» view (`App.tsx:1112`, `:1354`); the Board through the strip; the projects table, profile and journal under «Ещё» (CO-460); idle and installed agents in Настройки (`SettingsScreen.tsx` `agentsRoster`).
- A failed goals read keeps the older view (the task as the main line) and does not say «Цель не названа» (`agentGoals.ts` catch, `HomeInWork.tsx:138`); that the failure itself is not said on screen is acceptable while the task still shows.
- No em dash in any new string; English strings use English quotes (`git diff ... i18n`, `9fae41d6`).
- `docs/ux/2026-10-26-simple-fabric-v2.md` still says «Status: design, not built» although parts are built; that is the docs level's to rule on.

## Summary

- Blocking: 0.
- Not blocking: 11 (medium 5: UX-11 to UX-15; low 6: UX-16 to UX-21).
- Iteration 1's UX fixes: 9 of 10 hold; UX-3 regressed (UX-11).
- At a glance the simple Fabric answers «where is what being done»: projects and agents in the left column, «В работе» by project, the session opened only on press. What a person reads wrong is the agent's line: «Цель не названа» for an agent that took no task (UX-12), «Цель» for a local step (UX-13), and dropped work called «Передано» (UX-14). The one action that can do harm twice is the cross-project console's false failure (UX-15).
