## Not findings

- «Задачи» is newest first and above the history; each row opens in place to the brief and «Открыть задачу»; the history is closed by default and its rows name and open their task (P1; `AgentPage.tsx:142-213`).
- Reading, empty, failed with «Прочитать задачи снова», capped at 100 and a failed re-read that keeps the list are each said as the SCN-166 amendment states (P2, P3).
- An untitled current task is kept as «Задача без описания» on the agent page, Главная's agents and Агенты' rows (`AgentPage.tsx:138`, `HomeWork.tsx:249`, `AgentsScreen.tsx:97`).
- «Брошено: N» on Главная opens the Board in its kanban view (the home route carries no `needs`, `BoardScreen.tsx:69`), where «Брошено» stands right after «В работе» with its count and note (K1, K4).
- A claim row and a release row in the history name their task: their payload key is `work` (`eventSubject.ts`, writers in `workTools.ts:538`, `workRelease.ts:166,198,232`). My first probe used `work_id` and so showed no name; that was the probe's error.
- Russian plural forms of «брал … раз» are right for 3 and 21 (P1); the English plural rule uses `Intl.PluralRules` (`i18n/translate.ts:27-30`).
- `<details>` rows are native, so they open with Enter and Space and their content leaves the accessibility tree while closed; P1 shows them closed by default.
- A remembered agent or project filter on the Board can make «Брошено» show fewer cards than Главная's «Брошено: N»; the filter is on screen above the columns, as it already was for «В работе».
- No em dash in any new string (Copylot rule `no-em-dash` pass in both locales).

## Summary

- Blocking: 0.
- Not blocking: 10 (medium 3: UX-1, UX-2, UX-3; low 7: UX-4 to UX-10).
- The release does what the operator asked for an agent at work: its tasks newest first, expandable and openable, with every read state said. The two medium gaps are on the way in and back: the Board's link for dropped work opens no page (UX-1), and the agent is forgotten after opening one of its tasks (UX-2).
