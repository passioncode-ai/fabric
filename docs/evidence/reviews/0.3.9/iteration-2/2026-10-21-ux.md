| UX-1 | No | The stack-lost notice contradicts itself every 30 s while a hold stands. Introduced by the ER-3 hold, after iteration 1. | `apps/desktop/src/shared/stackWatch.ts:195-198` (`recoveryBegun` keeps `hold`), `:207` (held: next due in 30 s), `:283-285` (view: `trying` and `hold` both set); `apps/desktop/src/main/stackWatch.ts:142` (begun before `askContainers`), `:215-216` (publish after `tryRecover`); `renderer/src/StackHealthNotice.tsx:38-50`; `main/env.ts:161-162` (10 s timeout); probe above | Ask for the containers before `recoveryBegun`, or keep `trying` false for a re-check while a hold stands; in the view, never set `trying` when `hold` is set |
