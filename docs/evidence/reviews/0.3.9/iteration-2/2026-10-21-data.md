| DA-1 | No | **A responder whose lease lapsed counts as someone who can answer.** The three new "can anyone answer" tests use `holder_enrollment is not null`, while the board's own `board_responder_state` calls such a slot `stale` once `lease_until` passes. ## What holds

- **Migration 120 on the operator's data.** It applies in one transaction to the restored schema-94 copy, the ledger hash matches, `schema_version()` reads 95, and the first tick answers exactly the 4 requests a target replied to (journalled, idle on the second tick). The operator's board now has 8 queued requests: after the first tick 4 remain, 2 expiring 2026-10-26 (05:12Z, 07:45Z) and the 2 sent today on 2026-10-28; the CHANGELOG and plan figure "4 of the 6" stays true of the six it names.
- **`answered`.** The new check admits it, `apply_comms_requests` applies any `to`, `boardService.ts#requestState` and the tool descriptions carry it, and ADR-0149's amendment names it and `noResponder` as always-on extensions; no board tool declares an output schema a client would validate it against.
- **Task moves.** `task.moved@1` with `claim_id` and a `system` actor is applied by `apply_operating_surfaces` and `apply_move_provenance` with no actor or payload check that refuses it; `lastTaskMove` reads at most a 13 002-row journal.
- **`session.ended@1` reason `agent-left`.** Written once per session (`claimEndRecord`), with the hooks' `runtime` vocabulary; no projection consumes the event, so the new reason breaks no reader.
- **Claims renew** from any own or rejoin request at most every 5 minutes for claims within 15 minutes of expiry; the 2-minute sign keeps them.
- **The ops log at 48 agents** keeps about 213 KB an hour after sampling: the week is kept with room (17.6 days); `mcp.ping` is counted; failures, warnings, slow calls and stack transitions are written; `startup-failure.log` appends within 256 KB.
- **Measured numbers.** 86.9 % (22 outages, 22.0 h) recomputes exactly; "all 6 board requests ever sent were still queued" holds for the audit's time.
