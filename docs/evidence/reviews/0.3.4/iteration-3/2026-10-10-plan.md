## Findings

### PL-1 — non-blocking — ADR-0121 and ADR-0123 still place self-update and the CEO-chat retirement in "the release after 0.3.3" — that is 0.3.4, which carries neither

**Evidence.**
- ADR-0121's amendment 1 (`docs/adr/0121-…md:50-56`) says: "The target release becomes **the release after
  0.3.3**, and the copies that need one manual update are those up to and including **0.3.3**".
- ADR-0121's status line (`:5`) still says "target release 0.3.3".
- ADR-0123's amendment 1 (`docs/adr/0123-…md:52-60`) says: "becomes **the release after 0.3.3**".
- The index repeats both: `docs/adr/README.md:110` "target after 0.3.3 (amendment 1)", and `:111` "retirement after
  0.3.3, amendment 1".
- The plan moved both past 0.3.4 on 2026-10-10. P-12 (`backlog.md:120`) and P-13 (`:121`) say "planned after 0.3.4
  … its order with 0.3.5 … is not decided" and "≤ 0.3.4". No amendment records the move.
- `git log v0.3.3..06c5234c -- docs/adr/0121-* docs/adr/0123-*` → no commits.

**Why it matters.** The decision record and the plan now give different answers about what 0.3.4 contains. The
operator's 2026-10-10 call ("the fix ships now as 0.3.4, with universal macOS") is what took P-12 and P-13 out of
0.3.4, and it is recorded only in the plan. AGENTS rule 2 says a decision has one home, and a change to an ADR is a
new record. ADR-0123 is *accepted*, so a reader of the tag's ADRs is told that 0.3.4 retires the CEO chat. 0.3.3
iteration 1 found this same class of error (PL-1, V1-5).

It is non-blocking because the CHANGELOG, the plan and the shipped app are right; only the record lags. It cannot
go into the release commit either: `verifiedCandidateProblem` refuses any `docs/adr/` change after the verified
commit.

**Smallest fix.** In the close commit after publication, add "Amendment 2 — 2026-10-10" to ADR-0121 and to
ADR-0123:
- 0.3.4 is the fresh-install fix plus universal macOS (the operator's call);
- the target becomes "after 0.3.4", with its order relative to 0.3.5 not decided;
- for ADR-0121, "copies ≤ 0.3.4 need one manual update".

Also update ADR-0121's status line and index rows 110–111, and name all of this in *Release close* item 5.
