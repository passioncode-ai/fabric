## Findings

### UX-1 — non-blocking — The refusal's title says Fabric "opened" the Estate it could not open

- **Evidence.**
  - `apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/i18n/en.ts:905` reads "Fabric does not have access to the Estate it opened".
  - `ru.ts:284` reads «У Fabric нет доступа к пространству, которое он открыл».
  - The classifier's English title, written to `startup-failure.log` and copied by "Copy the details", is
    `shared/startupFailure.ts:103`.
  - The `docs/brand/strings.md:717` row has the same wording.
  - In this product "opens an Estate" means showing it (`activeEstate.ts:1–8`, "Which Estate this app opens"). Here
    no window opened, and the window title right above it says "Fabric could not start" (`en.ts:896`).
- **Why it matters.** The dialog's first line contradicts itself and the window title. A person reads that Fabric
  opened the Estate, yet has no window and no access. The brief's question is whether the sentence is honest, and
  this line is the one that is not quite.
- **Smallest fix I would accept.** "Fabric does not have access to the Estate it was opening" or "Fabric has no
  access to this Estate" (ru «…к пространству, которое он открывает»). Change it in the four places above. The
  remedy and the details stay as they are.

### UX-2 — non-blocking — When the repair could not *read* the database, the remedy still sends the person to file a report rather than retry

- **Evidence.**
  - The repair's own read failures return reasons such as "the estate's journal could not be read: …", "the
    membership could not be granted: …" and "the repair could not run: …" (`seedRepair.ts:79–93`).
  - These reasons are appended to a message that begins `that person is not a member` (`index.ts:663–667`), so the
    cause is `identity-refused` (`startupFailure.ts:100`).
  - Rendered, the details say `… did not apply: the estate's journal could not be read: fetch failed`, while the
    remedy says "this database does not name that owner … Copy them and report them". A passing database failure
    then reads as a defect to report, and the step that fixes it (Retry, the default button) is not named.
- **Residual from iteration 2.** Iteration 2's UX-2 part B, a chosen or restored Estate that refuses with no
  `active-estate.json` way back, was neither fixed nor ruled by V2-4. Today it is practically unreachable: a restore
  grants the person `owner` (migration 66, `:414`), and no surface revokes a membership. So I record it here rather
  than as its own finding.
- **Why it matters.** It is low reach: the database has to fail between two reads a few milliseconds apart. But it is
  the one rendered case where the remedy names the wrong next step.
- **Smallest fix I would accept.** One sentence in the remedy (en/ru/classifier): "If the details say something
  could not be read, retry first." Alternatively, rule it to a carry-over row.
