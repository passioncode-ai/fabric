## Checked and fine

- **PL-2 fixed:** `CEO_USES` now lists `ceoAgentStop` and `ceoAgentTakeCall`, and `ceoAgentVoiceReady` and `ceoAgentVoiceSample` are `MAIN_ONLY` and out of the CEO list.
- **PL-4 fixed:**
  - `personStopEnded` releases the hold when the other door's stop leaves the console running, and `terminal.end` and the History `stop` both call it.
  - `stop()` awaits `starting` and `pendingFallback`.
  - A start that finds `held` after `open` stops the console it opened.
  - There are tests for stop during start (ER-1), a message during stop (ER-2), a deferred fallback (ER-3) and the released hold (ER-6).
- **PL-6, key slot:** the `voiceReady` handler now uses `XAI_KEY_SLOT`. The deadline for the vault read and a real ru/en sample check are carried in CO-601.
- **PL-7 fixed:** `ceoCallWanted` is cleared when the CEO window closes.
- **PL-1:** history now lives in `localStorage`, so it survives closing the window, and the changelog says so. The edge of a call running at close is PL-9.
- **Stop/Restart:**
  - `held` is honoured in `ensure`, `onExit`, `onOutput`, `fallBack`, `startFallback` and `restart`. Only a person's restart, a message or Start clears it.
  - `status()` reports `stopped`.
  - Main-process tests simulate the pty exit-before-resolve order.
  - `CeoPopup.test.tsx` covers Stop, the stopped text, Start and the failed-stop message.
  - The i18n keys exist in both ru and en.
- **Existing call:**
  - `MAX_CALL_MS`, `IDLE_CALL_MS`, `CONNECT_DEADLINE_MS` and the generation guards are unchanged, and the protocol tests remain.
  - The key flow still goes through `VoiceKeyForm` and `setVoiceKey`, and `ASK_CEO_TOOL` is still in the tools.
  - Only `voice` changed, from `eve` to the character's voice. The mute and meter additions are optional on `CallAudio`.
- **Voice default and choice:**
  - No stored voice reads as male, and an unknown voice is dropped while the look is kept.
  - The mapping uses only xAI's five built-in voices.
  - The picker uses native radios, and the sample button is hidden without a key.
  - The first-run step appears only when `voiceReady`, and Settings always shows the choice.
- **Sample path:**
  - A whitelist check precedes the cache filename, so there is no path traversal.
  - The key goes in the request header only, and the test checks it does not leak.
  - The cache files are 0600.
  - The `VoicePicker` tests are real: default male, the character's voice, no key, a refused sample.
- **Call as a panel:** the panel sits under the console, shows a meter, time, price and who said what, has a text field and a voice-off switch, and keeps ten past calls. The home card opens the popup and starts the call there. The reduced-motion CSS is gated.
- **Docs:** the changelog, ADR-0197 amendment 2, SCN-184, SCN-186, SCN-187, the scenarios index, external-contracts and the CO-601 row agree with the code.
- **Not checked:**
  - `persona()`, `PersonaProvider`, `usePersona`, `useFabricName` and `FabricAvatar` are not in the material.
  - The persona save path in main and the variant-click handlers are not in the material.
  - The home card's render site is not in the material.
  - The CSS variables used (`--state-good`, `--state-running`, `--state-ended`, `--app-focus`, `--dur-quick`) were not confirmed to exist.
  - The policy test body was not seen.
  - No live xAI call or built-app walk was seen, apart from the design-map note that one was done.
