| ER-1 | blocking | A transient peer refusal from the hub makes fabric-mcp delete its binding credential. The new peer check answers `peer-unverified` with HTTP 403. ## Verdict
Blocking findings: 1

ER-1 is the one to fix before release. When the hub's new peer check says it cannot tell who is calling, it answers 403, and fabric-mcp treats any 403 as a revoked credential and deletes the agent's credential file. The cause can be as small as one slow `lsof` on a shared computer, and the agent's grants are lost with the file. I confirmed the client side with a probe. The hub side is read from code, since the agent-surface test needs a disposable stack.

The other eleven findings are non-blocking. The priority areas otherwise hold up:
- **No token leaves before the owner check.** `checkedFetch` writes the request head only after verify, and an abort or error during verify destroys a socket that carried nothing.
- **Dispatcher.** Records, retries, wake, stop and the record shape behave as specified.
- **Other modules.** I found no defect in the opsSink pre-bootstrap hold, the Colima `--activate=false` change, the board-digest backup check, or the unwired `callDoor` and `managerTools`.

All 332 targeted tests pass, none load-dependent.

On this machine (one human account, `lsof` 0.13 to 0.23 s) the ER-1 path needs an account-list failure first, so it is unlikely here but reachable on shared computers.
