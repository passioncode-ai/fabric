## Verdict
The candidate's person-visible changes work as tested. The ten UX-related test files (91 tests) pass, ru covers every key, and no new person-facing string carries an em dash. The scenario amendments for SCN-161 (Colima started with `--activate=false`), SCN-145 and SCN-132 (CO-506) and SCN-179 match the code. One release-note claim is false: CHANGELOG.md:37-39 quotes a «На проверке» row title that a later change replaced, and the scenario records that replacement. The release notes also leave out the largest person-visible additions of this release: the review decision card, the pre-start overlap note, the overlap decision and «Написать агенту». They also state the board rule in superseded wording. The other findings are wording, consistency and robustness gaps in the new screens, none of which breaks 0.3.16 behavior. Blocking findings: 1.
