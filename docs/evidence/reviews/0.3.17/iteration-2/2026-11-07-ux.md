## Verdict
The iteration 1 fixes at this level hold. The quoted «На проверке» title now matches the app, the review card's Russian agrees with its author, SCN-180 is consistent, ru covers every key, no new person-facing string has an em dash, and the 65 UX tests plus the obligations tests pass. Two blocking findings remain. First, the release-note bullet for the pre-start note, written as an iteration 1 fix, promises a check before every task or console start, naming the same task and the same backlog row. Only the project page checks, and no shipped caller can produce those two reasons. Second, the Board's detail pane lost the «Открыть» it gave a review in 0.3.16, while its card points to a history that is not there, against SCN-179's Board entry and step 5. Both fixes are small: reword one bullet, and render the open act beside the review card on the Board. The other four findings are scenario and notes precision. Blocking findings: 2.
