### solution
Keep full restore, mirror import, identity/membership and archive/purge separate task families; integrate through authority generation and read-only restored mode.

### decomposition
- Parent expands archive custody/manifest/projection rebuild matrix and fenced restoration.
- Parent expands auth provider/current membership/invitation return/single-use receipt.
- Add SCR-47/48 exact storage coverage and read-only restore display after backend contract.
- M77 reversible project archive first; true purge separate tombstone and receipt design.

### depends_on
- S02 boundary, AX-05 routes, operator resource choices for backup and auth transport

### acceptance_positive
- Mirror import remains atomic; restored environment read-only until explicitly activated.
- Authentication returns to exact still-valid invitation.

### acceptance_negative
- Old writers cannot mutate restored authority generation.
- Auth retry cannot consume second seat; purge cannot resurrect on replay.
- Do not label mirror as full backup.

### exclusions
- Do not repeat parent-owned storage/auth analysis; merge by existing owners.
